# Early Modern Recipe Books

Paleographic transcription and data analysis of early modern English recipe books, exploring gendered knowledge production, domestic medicine, and the construction of the recipe book genre.

## Data Sources

- **Folger Shakespeare Library** — one of the world's largest collections of early modern recipe books (130+ manuscripts)
- **Wellcome Collection** — digitized recipe books via FromThePage collaboration
- **Original photography** — manuscripts photographed during archival visits

## Methodology

1. Semi-diplomatic transcription of manuscript images using AI-assisted paleographic reading
2. Structured extraction of recipe data (ingredients, methods, categories)
3. Analysis and visualization of patterns across the corpus
