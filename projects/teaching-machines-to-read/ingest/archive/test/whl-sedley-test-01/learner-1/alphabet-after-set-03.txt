ALPHABET SNAPSHOT — after set 03 (pages 012–016)
Lady Sedley receipt book, single scribe, 1686. FINAL snapshot.
Extends alphabet-after-set-01 and -02. The hand is fully characterized; no hand-change
across all 15 pages. Set 3 (recipes XXIV–XLI) added more plague/cosmetic recipes and a
named physician attribution, and confirmed every habit noted earlier.

=== CARRIED FORWARD ===
All minuscule/majuscule forms, ligatures, and orthographic habits from snapshots 01 &
02 hold unchanged. This is a stable, regular round/italic late-17thC hand.

=== CONFIRMATIONS FROM SET 3 ===

[CONF] "ponder" = powder: the scribe writes "ponder" for "powder" repeatedly (pp.014
       Wormes recipe, p.015 knotts recipe). A consistent scribal spelling/slip, NOT a
       misreading. Preserved as "ponder".

[CONF] "see"/"soe"/"so": all three spellings of "so/thus" occur; also "see"=then. p.016
       shifts to spelling it "so" (e.g. "and so it will be well Healed", "as you shall
       so need"). The later plague pages (16) lean to "so"/"and" written in full and
       use "em" for "them" ("Mix em", "Stamp em").

[CONF] roman numerals as recipe headers run VI–XLI (with the scribe repeating/ skipping
       some, e.g. XXX used twice). Numerals transcribed as written; "iij","vij","xlj"
       use terminal-j. Also "14th"/"24" arabic ordinals in running text.

[CONF] superscript abbreviations: wth(with), yt(that/which), yu(you), yr(your),
       oyntmt(ointment), fundamt(fundament), Mr(Master). All silently lowered per
       Folger; forms of address (Mr) kept.

[CONF] ff = capital F: "ffloures", "ffiggs", "ffellons", "ffrankizinck". Rendered "ff".

[NEW] capital N, H in proper nouns ("Nasel"/"Hasel", "Hintent") — both initial loops;
      genuine N/H ambiguity in a couple of rare words (flagged).

[NEW] "peese"/"pease": p.016 uses a recurring noun "peese" (a pea-sized pellet / issue-
      pea); kept as written.

=== RESIDUAL DIFFICULTY (unchanged character) ===
The ONLY persistent difficulty across the whole book is rare materia-medica proper
nouns and a handful of place/person names and folk plant-names (e.g. Goats Rhine,
chissels, Sentron, bellange, Scribers, Masthar moniack, Boncle, Brittayne, Hintent,
Soats wind). The common English/recipe prose is read at high confidence. Where the
letterform and the obvious sense conflict (wines/veines, whore/place, person/pease),
the STROKE is transcribed and the sense flagged — bottom-up, per method.

=== CONFUSION RISKS (final) ===
- w / v initial (wines~veines, whore~where) — genuinely ambiguous; transcribe stroke.
- stone / stand / stanne (vessel words) — minim cluster.
- see / soe / so — context.
- ponder = powder (not a confusion — a scribal spelling).
- N / H initial in rare names.
