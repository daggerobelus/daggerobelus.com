PAGE 012 — two columns. LEFT (page "18"): plague preservative (XXIV), Drink for
Ricketts (XXV). RIGHT (page "19"): end of XXV, Medicine for Freckles (XXVI). Date
"1686" written centered at foot of right column.

LEFT FLAGS:
- "Sage of vertue Rue otherwise called herbe grace" : "herb grace" = rue (herb-of-
  grace). "Sage of vertue" = garden sage of virtue. ok.
- "use to drink this medicine" : ok.
- "& soe / together the quantity as a spoonefull" (line ~10-11) [together?]: "for the
  space of 9 dayes & soe ___ the quantity ___ a spoonefull at a time" — "together"
  reads oddly; possibly "& soe forwards" / "& take together". Flag.
- "this will say God help & preserve yu" [say?]: "this will (by) Gods help preserve" —
  "say" reads s-a-y but sense wants "by Gods help"; possibly "thro Gods help". Flag.

LEFT, XXV (Ricketts):
- "Scarious" = scabious. ok-ish; reads S-c-a-r-i-o-u-s. Flag light.
- "shuggar of each halfe" [shuggar?]: "Colts foot & Scarious shuggar of each halfe a
  handfull" — "shuggar"(sugar) odd in a handfull-measured herb list; possibly a herb
  or "shaggar". Flag.
- "the middle rhine of ash" = the middle rind/bark of ash. "rhine"=rind. ok-ish.
- "ferns rootes" : fern roots. ok.

RIGHT (XXV cont.):
- "Brook wind" (line 1) [Brook wind?]: "Water cresses, Brook wind of each one handfull"
  — possibly "Brook lime" (brooklime, a cress) — w/l ambiguity. Flag heavily — likely
  "Brook lime".
- "ffiggs sliced 10" : ff=capital F; "Figgs"(figs) sliced, 10 of them. ok.
- "Raisin of the sun stoned" = raisins of the sun, stoned. ok.
- "night of the 14th than to drinks" [than?]: "& also in the night of the 14th ___ to
  drinks you must put in" — "than"/"then to drinke". Flag light.

RIGHT, XXVI (Freckles):
- "pottle of the flowers of Rosemary" : ok.
- "lapp the glass in hay" : wrap the glass in hay. ok.
- "fennitory water" = fumitory water. f-e-n-n-i-t-o-r-y. ok-ish (flag light).
- "Plantin water" = plantain water. ok.
- "give it a warme over the fire" : warm it. ok.
- recipe otherwise clear.

Roman numerals XXIV–XXVI head the recipes; included as written. "1686" date included.
