A Most soveraigne and approved preservation
above all others, for the Purgeing away from the
Heart all evil humors which causeth the infection
of the Plague by one Mr Hintent
Doctor in Physick
XXXVII
Take the Root of double Crowfoot that beareth a double
yellow flower glistering like Gold, or instead thereof a
Clave of Garlick stamp it in a wooden dish, and fill
halfe a Nasel nutt shell full of the same, and tye the same
in the inn part neer to the Lower side of the left Arme
& handfull from the Wrest, and let it remaine there about
vij houres, then, open the same, and you shall find a
little Bladder in the same place, if it be not often inough
in the said 12 houres, let it remain untill the time that
the said Bladder be risen, and Cut off the Skin thereof
if the said Bladder be fallen, if it be not fallen prick it
with a pin, then Cut it of, and then take a peice of the
branch of an Elder tree of the greenest as bigg as a peese
pare of the bark, and tye the end thereof to the place
aforesaid, and three Ivy leaves uppon the same, first
prickd full of holes, and let it remain there so long
untill it have made a hole that will receive a peese, then
put therein a peese and lay thereon vij Ivie leaves
prickd, and so left the same with a peese and three Ivie
leaves, morning and evening and keep the same Clean
and every if dayes, as you shall so need, wash your
Arm with French Wines bloud warme, and do continue
with the same untill the danger of the Pestilence
be past.

The Order to heale the Same
XXXVIII
Fourteen dayes before you will heale the Same, you must
beware of Surfetting, and use no person nor Beanes, and
take halfe a pint of French wine and halfe a pint of Sallet
Oyle, and mingle them together, and bath the Sore therewith
twice or trice aday, and tye a Cloth wet therewith to the
Same; and so it will be well Healed

To make the Botch appear
XXXIX
Take a handfull of Sorrill otherwise cald grcen sawre
Stamp, and Straine it with a draught of Beere or Ale,
take a litle Treacle and the powder of one Nutmegg,
Mix em all together, give it the Sick to drink and the
Botch will appear within five hours after
probatum est

To break a Botch or Boyle
XL
Take Vnlimed Egrimony and Snails, Stamp em
together and Lay it to the sore.

Another for the Same
XLI
Take Raw honny, the Oyle of an egg & Wheat flower &
make a plaister thereof, Lay it to the Same.
