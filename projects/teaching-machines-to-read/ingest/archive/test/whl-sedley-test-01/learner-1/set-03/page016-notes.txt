  name; reads M-r H-i-n-t-e-n-t. Could be "Hinton"/"Hintent". Flag — proper name.
- "double Crowfoot ... glistering like Gold" = creeping/bulbous buttercup. ok.
- "a Clave of Garlick" [Clave?]: = a clove of garlic. reads C-l-a-v-e. Flag light
  (=Clove).
- "halfe a Nasel nutt shell" [Nasel?]: = a hazel nut-shell. reads N-a-s-e-l; likely
  "Hasel"(hazel). Flag — likely "Hasel".
- "in the inn part" [inn?]: "tye the same in the ___ part neer to the Lower side of the
  left Arme" — = "in the inner part". reads i-n-n. Flag light (=inner).
- "& handfull from the Wrest" : "a handfull('s breadth) from the wrist". "Wrest"=wrist.
  ok.
- "vij houres" : roman vij = 7. transcribed "vij".

  possible interlineal correction over "often". Read tentatively "often inough".

  flag — likely "pease"(pea, as in an issue-pea).

- "and every if dayes" (2nd-last line) [if?]: "morning and evening ... and every ___
  dayes, as you shall so need" — "if" uncertain; possibly "other"/"few". Flag.
