PAGE 014 — two columns. LEFT (page "22"): To Pickle Walnuts, "For the riseinge of the
Lyts" (XXX). RIGHT (page "23"): end of XXX, "A Receipt for the Wormes or a very good
Purge" (XXXI), worm/St-Anthony's-fire medicine (XXXII).

NOTE on numbering: "XXX" appears again here (it also appeared at page013 For a Tetter
as XXX). The scribe's numbering is irregular; transcribed as written each time.

LEFT FLAGS:
- "To Pickle Walnuts" : a culinary (not medical) receipt. Title clear.
- "at yr biggness that yu may runn a pin through" : when big enough to run a pin
  through. ok.
- "boyle the pickle up with wine / leaves" [wine leaves?]: possibly "vine leaves"
  (walnuts pickled with vine leaves) — w/v ambiguity. Flag — likely "vine leaves".
- "rockd allom" = roche alum. ok.
- "& boyle yr wth salt" (line 9) [yr?]: "& boyle ye(it) wth salt". "yr"->ye/it.
  Flag light.
- "scrape the inwards out with a sliver" [sliver?]: possibly "skewer"/"sliver"(a
  splinter of wood). reads s-l-i-v-e-r. Flag light.
- "power the pickle to them" = pour. ok.

LEFT, XXX (riseinge of the Lyts):
- "the Lyts" [Lyts?]: "the riseinge of the Lyts" — = "the lights" (the lungs);
  "rising of the lights" is a known complaint. reads L-y-t-s. Flag — likely "Lights".
- "Tiffany" = tiffany (a thin gauze). ok.
- "an Amber Collour" : ok.

RIGHT (XXX cont.):
- "to the partie when the fitt cometh" : ok ("to" superscript inserted, lowered).
- "if it doth not Lay it" [Lay?]: "if it doth not ___ it give two spoonefulls more" —
  possibly "stay it"/"allay it". reads L-a-y. Flag.
- "sundry fitts" : ok.

RIGHT, XXXI (Wormes/Purge):
- "ponder" (x4) = powder. The scribe writes "ponder" for "powder" repeatedly here
  ("must be ponder of hartshorne", "ponder this Curralline to ponder"). Preserved as
  written ("ponder"); flag light (consistent scribal spelling/slip).
- "must be ponder of hartshorne" (line 2) : possibly dittography/garble — "& 2 peny
  worth of hartshorne & must be ponder of hartshorne". Preserved; flag light.
- "Curralline" = coralline (a seaweed vermifuge). c-u-r-r-a-l-l-i-n-e. ok-ish.
- "worme seed" = wormseed. ok.
- "wash your mouth wth an other spoonefull" : ok.

RIGHT, XXXII (heate in the face / St Anthony's fire):
- "for any heate in the face or other whore" (title line 2) [whore?]: "heate in the
  face or other ___, although it be St Anthonys fire" — "whore" reads w-h-o-r-e but
  sense wants "place" or "sore" or "where". Flag heavily — letterform vs sense.
- "St Anthonys fire" = erysipelas. ok.
- "smithy water" [smithy?]: "a pottle of ___ water" — smith's (forge-quench) water is
  a real remedy ingredient; reads s-m-i-t-h-y. ok-ish. Flag light.
- "the ground roses of it" (line ~2 of XXXII) [ground roses?]: "Elder leaves or the
  ground ___ of it" — uncertain; possibly "ground rootes"/"young roots". Flag.
- "& 2 ounces sooth / all these" [sooth?]: "& 2 ounces ___ all these from a pottle to a
  pint" — possibly "seeth"(boil) — s-o-o-t-h vs s-e-e-t-h. Flag — likely "seeth".
- "gally pott" = gallipot. ok.
- "anoynt his face wth it" : ok.
- "(by Gods grace) help." : ok.

Roman numerals XXX–XXXII as written.
