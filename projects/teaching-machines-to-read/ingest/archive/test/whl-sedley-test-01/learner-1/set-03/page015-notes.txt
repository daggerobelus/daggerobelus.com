PAGE 015 — two columns. LEFT (page "24"): red rubies/face (XXXIII), naturally red
face (XXXIV), disolve knotts in the Neck (XXXV). RIGHT (page "25"): end of XXXV,
"A Medicine for the Plague" (XXXVI). This page is comparatively clear & well-spaced.

LEFT FLAGS:
- "red rubies that grows in the face" : "rubies" = red pimples/couperose. ok.
- "hoggs sewet" = hog's suet. "sewet" spelling. ok.
- "water distilled of a peach tree & ffloures" : peach-tree & flowers distilled water.
  "ffloures"=flowers (ff). ok.
- "an oyntmt" = ointment (abbreviated "oyntm't" superscript t lowered). ok.
- XXXIV "the redish" = the reddish (redness). ok.
- "Gourd Seed" : ok.

LEFT, XXXV (knotts in the Neck) — gum/resin list:
- "Masthar moniack" (line ~5) [Masthar moniack?]: = "Gum Ammoniac" (Gummi Ammoniacum).
  reads M-a-s-t-h-a-r m-o-n-i-a-c-k; almost certainly "Sarcocoll" or "Ammoniack".
  Flag heavily — likely "...Ammoniack".
- "Olibanum" = olibanum (frankincense). ok.
- "Galbanum" = galbanum. ok.
- "ffrankizinck" [ffrankizinck?]: = "Frankincense". ff + reading f-r-a-n-k-i-z-i-n-c-k.
  Flag — likely "ffrankinsence".
- "Cummin" = cumin. ok.
- "in ponder" = in powder (scribe's "ponder" for powder, cf. page014). preserved.
- "clarifyd Wax" : ok.
- "iij ounces" : roman numeral with terminal j (=3). transcribed "iij". ok.

RIGHT (XXXV cont.):
- "in the Coling" = in the cooling. ok.
- "into rowls" = into rolls. ok.
- "on sheeps Leather" : sheep's leather. ok.

RIGHT, XXXVI (Plague medicine) — mostly clear:
- "herb grace" = rue. ok.
- "safe for 24 dayes" (line ~7) [24?]: "after the first Spoonefull they shall be safe
  for ___ dayes and after the 14th" — number ambiguous, reads like "24" but could be
  "14"; the following "after the 14th" suggests the first figure may also be "14".
  Flag — number uncertain.
- "water of Brittayne" [Brittayne?]: "so much water of ___" — uncertain; possibly
  "water of Betony"/"of Bryony"(briony water). reads B-r-i-t-t-a-y-n-e. Flag heavily.
- "a quantity of fine Boncle" (line ~3 of 2nd para) [Boncle?]: very uncertain — "a
  quantity of fine ___, put all together". Possibly "Bole"(bole armeniac) / "Treacle".
  reads B-o-n-c-l-e. Flag heavily.
- "the boyle to appeare" = the bubo/boil. ok.
- "afforesaid" = aforesaid. ok.
- "by the Grace of God" : recurs; clear.

Roman numerals XXXIII–XXXVI as written.
