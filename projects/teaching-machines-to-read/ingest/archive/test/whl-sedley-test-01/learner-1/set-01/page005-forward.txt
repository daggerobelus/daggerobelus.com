Palsy Water

of Lillys Convally, Cowslipps of each one handfull,
of Sage flowers & Rosemary flowers & Lidiany flower
of each halfe a handfull let all those be gathered in their
severall seasons for they are not to be had all at one
time, but take the first that cometh & let them be steep-
ed in very good spirit of wine distilled from the as
phlegme in two glasses till all may be had, And let them
stand some dayes after the last is in, & not always very close
stopped, & see when Lavender cometh stripp of the fairest
heads the quantity of 2, & put them into a large gally
glass & pour on them as much spirit of wine as before
& cover them well stopped & let them stand 14 dayes
