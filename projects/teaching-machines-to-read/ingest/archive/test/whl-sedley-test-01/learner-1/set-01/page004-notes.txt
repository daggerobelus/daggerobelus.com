PAGE 004 — two columns. LEFT: "To make Snaile water" (page no. "2" top-left).
RIGHT: continuation of Snaile water, then "Aqua Mirabilis" (page no. "3").

Reading order: full LEFT column top-to-bottom, then full RIGHT column top-to-bottom.

LEFT COLUMN FLAGS:
- "for the and" (line 10) [?]: reads "take them for the and" — looks like a false
  start / dittography; the scribe may have written "for the" then "and". Preserved as
  seen. Possibly "take them forth and" with -th miswritten. Flag — revisit.
- "as which fott" (line 18) [as which fott?]: troublesome. Above-line "as" appears
  cancelled/inserted. "fott" may be "sett" or "putt" or "into". The clean Iron pot is
  set into a Limbeck/still. Best trace: "as which fott yr Limbeck". Low confidence on
  "fott". Flag heavily — revisit.
- "boarse" (line 20, end) [boarse?]: after "rosemary flowers allsoe ___" — a herb.
  reads b-o-a-r-s-e. Could be "Borrage"/"borage" or "hoarse". Flag.
- "Roddock roots" [Roddock?]: r-o-dd-o-c-k. A plant root (madder? "Roddock"?). Flag.
- "fenagrick" [fenagrick?]: fenugreek. reads f-e-n-a-g-r-i-c-k. Flag spelling.
- "Tunricks of sack" [Tunricks?]: very uncertain. t-u-n-r-i-c-k-s. Possibly
  "Turmerick"/"Turnericks" (turmeric). Strokes ambiguous. Flag heavily — revisit.
- "Rew" = rue (herb). r-e-w. ok.
- "Bettony" = betony. clear.
- "Sallendine" = celandine; recurs right column & Aqua Mirabilis. scribe's spelling.
- "secure them with water" (line 14) [secure?]: s-e-c-u-r-e; means to cover/scour the
  worms with water. Possibly "scoure". Flag.

RIGHT COLUMN FLAGS:
- "pleafs" (line 2) [pleafs?]: = "please"; long-s + terminal s read as "pleafs".
  Could be "pleafe". Flag.
- "eyngermost" (line 7) [eyngermost?]: very uncertain — "it must be ___most". Possibly
  "vppermost" or "lyngermost". Strokes don't resolve. Flag heavily — revisit.
- "at gries" (line 17) [gries?]: "& at gries comeing from the obstructions" — perhaps
  "agues" or "griefe". reads g-r-i-e-s. Flag.
- "the peny waight of saffron" : "peny waight" = pennyweight. ok-ish; "waight"
  spelling preserved.
- "Bee or of wine": looks like abbreviated "Beere"; reads "Bee" with flourish. Flag light.
- "to be use two spoonefulls": gramm7atically "to be used"; reads "use". Preserved.
- aqua vitæ: æ ligature in "vitæ" — rendered "vitæ".

RIGHT COLUMN, Aqua Mirabilis:
- "Cardimum, Cubebs, Gallingall, Melilote flowers" — spice/herb list, mostly clear.
  "Cubebs" clear; "Gallingall" (galingale) clear; "Melilote" clear.
- "Cardimum" [Cardimum?]: cardamom; c-a-r-d-i-m-u-m. Flag spelling.
- "beings made into powder": "beings" = being; reads b-e-i-n-g-s. Preserved.
- title "Aqua Mirabilis" clear; page number "3" beside it.

GENERAL: "&" connector throughout = ampersand. "yu"=you, "yr"=your, all superscript
graphs lowered. "putt"/"lett"/"putt" doubled-t habit consistent.
