A good preservative against the plague
or pestilence
XXIV
Take Sage of vertue Rue otherwise called herbe
grace, Elder leaves & redd bramble leaves of each
of them a good handfull stamp them together
& then straine them through a fine Linnen cloth
and put to the Iuice a quart of good whitewine
and a good quantity of whitewine vinegar
mingle them together & put hereto a quarter
of an ounce of white Ginger beaten to small
powder use to drink this medicine every morn-
ings fastings for the space of 9 dayes & soe
together the quantity as a spoonefull at a time
and this will say God help & preserve yu for the
space of a whole yeare

A Drink for the Ricketts
XXV
Take of Bettany Egrimony, Hartstongue, Liver-
worte, Colts foot & Scarious shuggar of each halfe a
handfull, the middle rhine of ash, & ferns rootes
of each a quarter of an ounce, Water cresses
Brook lime of each one handfull, ffiggs sliced 10, Raisin
of the sun stoned 3 ounces, boyle all those in a pottle of
faire water unto a quart & when it is almost boyled
put in a quarter of an ounce of Liquorish strain'd it &
lett her drink of it 3 or 4 times a day & also in the
night of the 14th than to drinks you must put in
hartshorne rasped halfe an ounce

A Medicine for Freckles
XXVI
Take a quart of white wine & a pottle of the flowers
of Rosemary put them in a good glass bottle, then fill
a great skillett with water lapp the glass in hay & sett
it in the skillett before yu sett it on the fire & soe lett
it grow very warme but not boyle at all, then take a pint
of white wine vinegar put a new laid egg in it and lett
it remaine in the vinegar till the egg be consumed
or disolved then straine the vinegar & whitewine &
soe put the liquor in a clean glass, then give it
a warme over the fire & soe keep it for yr use
Take a pint of Plantin water & a pint of fennitory
water, drinke a quarter of a pint every morning &
and fast an houre after it
1686
