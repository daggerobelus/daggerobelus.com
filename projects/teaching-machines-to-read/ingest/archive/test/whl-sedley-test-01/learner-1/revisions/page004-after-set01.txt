To make Snaile water
Take a good peck of garden snayles in their shells
& wash them in a great bowle of beere, then make
your Chimney hearth very cleane & poure out half
a bushell of Charcoales and set them on fire, & when
they are throughly kindled, then with a fire shovell
make a great hole in the midst of them & poure the
snayles & scatter some fire of yr quick coales amongst
them, & see to them rest till they have done makeing
a noise, And then you must take them forth and
with a knife & course cloth pick & wipe them
from the ashes & coles the green froth that is upon
them. Then in a stone Mortar bruise them shells &
all, take allsoe a quart of earth wormes, & secure them
with water very well divers times over, then wash them
soe from their filth, then put them into a stone Mortar
& beat them well to peeces, then take a clean Iron pot & put
as will fitt yr Limbeck or still, then take 2 handfulls
of Sallendine & lay in the bottome of yr pott & 2 hand-
fulls of Angelica must be laid upon that, then put
in a Quart or two of rosemary flowers allsoe course
Agrimony, Burdock roots, Barke of Barbary trees, wood
Sorrell, Bettony, & sack of them two handfulls, halfe
a handfull of Rew of fenagrick & Turmerick of sack
an ounce well beaten, then lay yr snayles & wormes
upon the top of all those hearts and flowers
Then pour in three galls of the strongest Ale, then Cover
your pott & lett it stand all night & longer if yu please
in the place where yu meane to make yr fire under it.
In the morninge yu must put in three ounces of Cloves
beaten to powder, the peny waight of saffron dryed to powder
Harts horne fyled six ounces, yu must not stir it after
yu have put in the hartshorne for it must be vppermost
then sett your limbeck & Closse it with Rye dough & make
a very fast fire under it & receive yr water by pint this
first is strongest the next weaker & weaker, but the weakest
is usefull, the smallest if need be may be helped with putting
in one spoonefull of the strongest to 4 of the smallest; It is
to be use two spoonefulls of the strongest to 4 of ale or Beere
or with wine, & the snayles as much of Ale or Bee or of
wine, This water is by experience good for the yellow
Jaundise, & at gries comeing from the obstructions
of the Liver helps the Consumption, it must be taken in
the morning & two howres before made in the after-
noons with some moderate stirrings of the body
after the takeings of it &c

Aqua Mirabilis
Take of the seedes of Cardimum, Cubebs, Gallingall
Melilote flowers, Cloves, Mace, Nutmeggs ginger of each
one dramme, beings made into powder putt them into
3 pints of white wine & one pint of aqua vitæ, a pint of
the Iuice of Sallendine putt these into a glass Still and
soe lett them incorporate all night, in the morning distill it
