## set 01 revision round — page003, line 7 & 19
flag: [stanne?]
before: a stanne of 4 Galls ... stanne of Ale or Beere
after:  a stanne of 4 Galls ... stanne of Ale or Beere  (unchanged; still [stanne?])
why:    Re-read with matured alphabet. Strokes remain s-t-a-nn-e (clear 'a' + double
        minim). A 4-gallon standing vessel; "stanne"/"stand" plausible. No clearer
        reading available. Leaving as transcribed; flag stays open.

## set 01 revision round — page003, line 9
flag: [peeeces?]
before: into little peeeces
after:  into little peeces
why:    On re-read the apparent triple-e is two e's with a connecting ligature stroke,
        not dittography. Resolves to "peeces" (the scribe's normal spelling, cf.
        page004 "beat them well to peeces"). Revised.

## set 01 revision round — page004, line 10
flag: [for the and?]
before: then you must take them for the and
after:  then you must take them forth and
why:    With the hand better known, the cramped "for the" is "forth" (f-o-r-t-h, the
        terminal -th with the looped h limb). "take them forth and / with a knife" reads
        cleanly. Revised.

## set 01 revision round — page004, line 18

before: as which fott yr Limbeck or still
after:  as will fitt yr Limbeck or still

        "take a clean Iron pot ... as will fitt yr Limbeck or still" (a pot that fits
        the still). "fott" resolves to "fitt" (doubled-t habit). Still slightly
        uncertain on "as will"; revised, flag narrowed but kept.

## set 01 revision round — page004, line 20
