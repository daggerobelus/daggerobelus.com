To Pickle Walnuts
Take yr Walnuts at yr biggness that yu may runn a pin
through & put them in a pot & cover them wth a pickle
made of salt & water, boyle the pickle up with wine
leaves lett them lye a weeke or a fortnight in the
pickle if you like it, if they mould put a peece of
rockd allom in & cover them close, then take to a
pint of whitewine a quart of whitewine vinegar
& boyle yr wth salt & lett the pickle be cold, then
take the nutts out of the pickle & cross them with
a knife & scrape the inwards out with a sliver
and fill them with mustard, pepper, Ginger, and
garlick mixt all together, Lay them close together
in the pott & power the pickle to them, Cover them
with a slate or Tile //

For the riseinge of the Lights
XXX
Take red rose water a quarter of a pint or also a
peny worth of Saffron tyed up in a Lawne bagg or
Tiffany steep it in water a while, then crush it betweene
two spoones till yu make the water an Amber Collour
then give two spoonefulls of it, to the partie when the fitt
cometh & if it doth not Lay it give two spoonefulls more
immediately for three or four sundry fitts

A Receipt for the Wormes
or a very good Purge
XXXI
Take 4 peny worth of Alloes & 2 peny worth of worme
seed & 2 peny worth of hartshorne & must be ponder
of hartshorne & one peny worth of Curralline, bruise
the Alloes & pound the wormes seed very small & ponder
this Curralline to ponder then mingle them together
give it 3 mornings together as much as will lye on a
six pence in a spoonefull of Beere & then wash your
mouth wth an other spoonefull of Beere & fast 2 howres
after it, & then take a litle thin broth & keep yr selfe
warme, be sure yu doe not get cold

A very good Medicine for any heate in the face
or other where, although it be St Anthonys fire
XXXII
Take a pottle of smithy water a handfull of sage, 2 hand-
fulls of Elder leaves or the ground roses of it, & 2 ounces seeth
all these from a pottle to a pint, put it in an earthen or gally pott
lett yr patient anoynt his face wth it when he goeth to bed in the
next morninge he shall find ease, but in 5 or 6 dayes (by
Gods grace) help.
