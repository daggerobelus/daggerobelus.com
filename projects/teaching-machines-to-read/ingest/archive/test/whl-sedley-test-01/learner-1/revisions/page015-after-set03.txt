To take away red rubies that grows in the
face by reason of the heat of the Liver
XXXIII
Take hoggs sewet well purifyed & brimstone as much
of the one as of the other wth water distilled of a
peach tree & ffloures & incorporate all together wth a
slow fire made thereof an oyntmt in the meane time
abstaine from wine & all hott things.

for him that hath naturaly a red face
XXXIV
Take 4 ounces of Kernells of Peaches 2 ounces Gourd
Seed and make thereof an Oyle wherewith you may
annoynt his face Morning & Evening, and this will
destroy the redish.
A thing found true by Experience.

To disolve knotts in the Neck
XXXV
Take clarifyd Wax iij ounces, Cleare Turpentine one
ounce, Rosin 2 ounces, Pitch one ounce, Masthar moniack
Mastick, Olibanum, a penny worth of each, Galbanum
ffrankinsence, and Cummin, in ponder of each one peny
worth, dissolve the Gums in Wine vinegar all night
then put them to your other things and melt them
wth a gentle fire stirring them often, then straine them
and in the Coling put in the Turpentine, and worke it
into rowls, & when you use it Spread it on sheeps
Leather, Apply it to the place and every Morning
wipe it and lay it on againe.

A Medicine for the Plague
XXXVI
Take a handfull of Sage a handfull of herb grace
a handfull of Elder Leaves, a handfull of red brambles leavs
and stamp them together, Strayne them in a Cloath wth a
Quart of white wine, take a quantity of Ginger and mingle
them together, drink thereof Evening and Morning a full
Spoonefull every day 30 dayes together, after the first Spoon-
full they shall be safe for 24 dayes and after the 14th
they shall be safe for the whole yeare, by the Grace of
God.
And if it fortune, one to be stricken with the Plague, or he
have drunken of the medicine afforesaid, then take water
of saffron a spoonefull, and so much water of Brittayne a
quantity of fine Boncle, put all together and it will put
out the venome; And if it fortune the boyle to appeare
then take Brambles leaves & Elder leaves, & mustard seeds, Stamp
them all together, then lay it on and make thereof a plaister
& lay it to the sore, it will draw out the venome, and the
Patient shall be whole by the Grace of God.
