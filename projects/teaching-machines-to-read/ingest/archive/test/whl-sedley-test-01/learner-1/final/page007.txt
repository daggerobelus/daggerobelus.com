To make oyle of Swallowes
Take 10 or 12 younge Swallowes ready to fly stamp them
them in a stone Mortar with their quills & feathers together
with Lavender, Cotten, Balme, Spearmint, Sedage, Camemill
wild strawberries leaves & roote thyme of each a handfull bea-
ten these hearbs, the one day unmashed after they be gathered
then stamp all together with the Swallowes till noe feathers
be left whole, put thereto a good quantity of May butter
or fresh butter & sett all those very softly three quarters
of an houre or a whole house, then strain it through a
cleane Linen Cloath into a Cleane vessell & keepe it
for your uses.

To Make a Jelly of Hartshorne
VII
Take a pint & a halfe of faire runing water & halfe a
pint of white wine, two ounces of hartshorne scraped let it
boyle softly in a pipkin close covered till it come to a pint, then make
it is hot strain it & put to it a litle slicst ginger & Cinament suld
thro next day take of yt topp of it & leave it to thicke of it if yt may
have it cleared & put it in a skellitt & put to it a suggar a topp of
sweet Marjerom, halfe a spoonefull of see rose water, a litle Juce of a
Lemon & an Orange, & soe let it stone a litle while upon yr fire, & put
through a jelly bagg or a litle rosemary springs in 4 bottoms of yr bagg
take it morning & evening & 2 or 3 times in a day in cole or
ells melt it, & soe drink it bloud warme //
For the dropsy, yellow Jaundice, the Hectic breakings
out & Mangies of body to purifye it blood from corrup-
tion & green sickness & all stoppings, very good for young
maids
VIII
Take the rootes of Monkes Rubarbe otherwise called patience
& seed Madder of each halfe a pint, 4 ounces of chissels and
Lickoris of each 2 ounces, Scabious & Egrimony of each one
handfull, slice the roots of Rubarb, then & chissels and
Lickoris bruise & hearbs with yt hand, put them into a
pott called a stand with 3 galls of strong Ale, to stepp
the space of three dayes then drink this as your ordina-
ry liquor for the space of 3 weeckes at the least, then every
you take it the better provided you have one stand
under another //
