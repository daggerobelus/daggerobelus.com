A Receipt for the cure of an Ague
XII
Take Elder Stickes of one yeares shoot newly cut from the tree
Scrape of the upper bark first which is naught, then scrape of
the green inward barke & of that take a good handfull & bruise
in a stone Mortar & receive bloud then put it into a pint of strong
Ale and let it infuse all night before the fire, the pott be covered
soe that it may be warme and not more and a little before the
partye expects this fitt straine the Ale from the barke, the last
beings squeezd is the strongest and best, & put into it better than
halfe a spoonefull of pepper grosly beaten, and see soone as they
find any grudgeing of the fitt drink it and stirr up and
downe, and in three takeing it will Cure
It hath been very often proved

To make Pills for the head & Stomach
XIII
Take an ounce of Aloes, a quarter of an ounce of Rubarb, halfe
a quarter of an ounce of cinnamum Rasitum, beate these into
fine powder & make those into pills mingled with the
Compound of Syrrup of wormwood, take three of them
halfe an houre before Supper, and see Continue every third
night till the whole Masse be taken
Probatum
After the pills have wrought drink some possett drinke
about two hours after
A Drink Good for the Palsy
XIV
Take of the shavings of the Stalks of Rosemary halfe a
weight Orange peels, sliced barke of oak one handfull, of
Sage dryed by the fire & sliced one halfe yu handfulls, put these in 2 or
3 quarts of new clarett into a bellange bagg & hang it in a vessell
of 4 or 5 gallons of Ale or beere of a midle strength & drink no
noe other drinke for a whole yeare

Pils for the head
XV
Take of Aloes 2 dram, the roots of wild gaures of all sorts of
Mirabilans, Diagredeum, Mastick, Bayberries, Leves of each
halfe a drame of saffron a scruple stamp them well together
with the Iuice of Coleworts & take seaven pills of the same
Morninge and Evenings

A Medicine for the Stone
XVI
Take the seeds of Saxifrage rootes dryed to powder three
times in a weeke, every day as much of the powder as
will lye upon a six pence in white wine, the next weeke
drink it twice in the weeke, the third weeke once,
see this every Quarter of the yeare
