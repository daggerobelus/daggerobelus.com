A Powder
Take of Corrall Amber and Jett of each one ounce, then
take 3 ounces of Pearle & 6 ounces of hartshorne burnt
with it, take the smallest reedes of the eggs & a litle
black claw of the king Crabb of each halfe an ounce, the gall
if possible must be taken when the signe is in Cancer take
a quantum ounce of the Stones, & yt shall find in the heads
of the Crawfish & take halfe an ounce of yt bones when
are in a stagg heart which must be burnt, halfe an ounce
of Indian Snak wood, as much of yt rootes of Goats
Rhine which must be gathered in winter & well dryed
take all those ingredients & beat them into very fine
powder & grind them with redress water, upon a
painters stone & let it dry, then powder it very
fine, & soarce it through a very fine Lawne yt if it
will not come through beat & grind againe because yt
may not loss any of the Substance, they must be
burnt & soarced severally, & when yu have made them
into fine powder, then weigh them againe, & take of
the Corrall Amber & Jott of equall quantities & of the
Pearle & hartshorne what they yield when they are equally
soarced then take 2 ounces of rasped hartshorne, & put
it into a pipkin with 3 pints of runings water & boyle
it till it come to a stiff Jelly & when yu think it enough
take a Spoonefull & let it Coole & if it be stiff strain it
the very, & take 4 penyworth of saffron dry it into powder and
straine it into yr Jelly whilst it is hott & soe let it coole
then put all yr powders into an Allablaster Mortar & your
Jolly by little & little, untill it come to a past, and if you
please yu make it up into balls or into the fashion of
an egg, or into long rovells, yu must put in the seede
wood, Goats Rhine rootes & an ounce of bearstone, halfe
an ounce of Amber greace & 20 graines of Musk, your
Musk & Amber greace must be powdered & put into the
Jelly

A Pill for the stone in the
kidneys & Collick
XI
Take Holland powder 2 dramms Pulvis sanctus one-
dram & a halfe Pulvis ad Calicum 4 scruples, powder
Mint, powder of Licoris, of each one drams, Balsom
Indus, one scruple, venice Turpentine washed very well
as much as will bring all into a Masse to pills
Take mornings & evenings 3 at a time when yu fitt
is about 10 times or more in a month, and when
the fitt is on yu take 4 or 5 pills & yu faithfull drink
as much Ale yu can possible, yu yu vomit drink as
more Ale as yu can & either walk or ride 2 or 3 howrs
after it and this will bring away the stone and
remove the Collick
Take some of those pills every month, it will
prevent the diseass from comeing againe //
