A receipt to make black Salve
XVII
Take one quart of Linseed oyle & halfe a pound of Bees-
wax, halfe a pound of verry good white lead otherwise
called Ceros, ffirst put the oyle into a skillet and let
the oyle be moderate hott, then add to it the wax let
them seeth together until the wax be quite melted
then put in the Ceros, as soone as yu have put in the
Ceros stirr them altogether, & lett them boyle near about
two houres & lett it be stirred all that time, it must be
boyled till it be black, & stiffe and smell well, and that it
will rowle betweene yr fingers
It must be a very moderate heat of wood Coles

A recipt for Tooth Ache
XVIII
Take a pint of whitewine vinegar, take the quantity of
3 doors glasses of the purest venice glass, put all yr Venice
glasse into the fire where you must burn it untill such
time as it be ready to disolve to nothings, then take it out
of the fire & put it into the redest vinegar, & doos of it & soe
burned it will melt & disolve to nothing haveing prepared you
Liquor in this manner, & onset when yu are troubled the
tooth Ache take some thereof & warme it in a dish or porringer
then take a moutfull none & if soe it in yr mouths as long as you
can rest swallowing, for to swallow much on't would be as bad as poyson
To make a healing Salve
XIX
Take halfe a pd of Sheeps suett, halfe a pd of the
best Rosin, halfe a pd of Beeswax, and 3 peny
worth of Venice Turpentine, first melt the hard
then put in the Turpentine, & lett it but just boyle
up, and soe rowle it up.

To make oyle of Charity
XX
Take Rosmary, red sage, wormewood, Lavender,
the lesser valerian, Camomill of each one handfull put
these into a quart of the best Sallett oyle, sett it in
the Sunn seaven dayes, then sett it on the fire and lett
it but boyle, then straine it from the hearbs, & putt in
fresh herbs, & soe doe 3 times & power of the cleare from
the thick & keep it for yr use.
This is good for all bruises to be taken inward or outward
if inward give 2 spoonefulls of the cleare in a little
balme posset drink, & annoynt it outward with the
thicke, this will help yr Toothache & all Cole Rhumes if yu
but dipp a cloath & lay it on, A litle of this oyle in
Scribers oyle will cleanse a wound
