PAGE 008 — two columns. LEFT (page "10"): "A Powder" (X). RIGHT: end of the powder
recipe, then "A Pill for the stone in the kidneys & Collick" (XI).

This is a hard page — many materia-medica/animal ingredients, cramped hand.

LEFT FLAGS:
- title "A Powder" with crossed "X" beneath (recipe number X). ok.
- "reedes of the eggs" [reedes?]: "the smallest reedes of the eggs" — uncertain;
  possibly "seedes". Flag.
- "the gall / if possible must be taken when the signe is in Cancer": astrological
  timing; "gall" ok; "signe is in Cancer" clear.
- "a quantum ounce of the Stones" [quantum?]: possibly "a quarter ounce". Flag.
- "yt shall find in the heads / of the Crawfish" : "wch shall find in the heads of the
  Crawfish" (crab-stones from crayfish heads). "yt" maybe "wch". Flag light.
- "yt bones when / are in a stagg heart" : the bone in a stag's heart (os cordis). ok.
- "Indian Snak wood" = snakewood. ok.
- "Goats Rhine" (x2) [Rhine?]: "rootes of Goats Rhine" — possibly "Goats Rue" or a
  root name. reads R-h-i-n-e. Flag heavily.
- "redress water" [redress?]: "grind them with ___ water, upon a painters stone" —
  uncertain; possibly "rose water" or "fresh water". Flag.
- "painters stone" = a painter's grinding stone (porphyry slab). ok.
- "soarce / soarced" = to sarse/sieve (pass through a sarse). recurs. ok.
- "loss any of the Substance" = lose. ok.
- "rasped hartshorne" = rasped/grated. ok.
- "strain it / the very" (line ~25) [the very?]: uncertain trailing — possibly "strain
  it thorough" / "the more". Flag.
- "Allablaster Mortar" = alabaster mortar. ok.
- "Jolly" (line 29) : the scribe wrote "Jolly" for "Jelly" (J + o); preserved as written
  (matches "Jelly" elsewhere — possible slip). Flag light.
- "to a past" = to a paste. ok.

RIGHT FLAGS:
- "long rovells" [rovells?]: "into long rovells" — possibly "rowells"/"rolls". reads
  r-o-v-e-l-l-s. Flag.
- "the seede / wood" [seede wood?]: possibly "snake wood" (cf. left col). Flag.
- "bearstone" [bearstone?]: "an ounce of ___" — possibly "beazerstone"/"bezoarstone"
  (bezoar). reads b-e-a-r-s-t-o-n-e. Flag heavily — likely bezoar stone.
- XI title "A Pill for the stone in the kidneys & Collick" with "XI". ok.
- "Pulvis sanctus", "Pulvis ad Calicum [?]", "Balsom Indus", "venice Turpentine":
  latin/apothecary names. "ad Calicum" uncertain (ad colicam?). Flag.
- "Holland powder" : a named powder. ok.
- "when yu fitt / is about 10 times" : "when yr fitt is about 10 times or more in a
  month". "yu"->"yr". ok.
- "yu faithfull drink" (line ~9 right) [faithfull?]: uncertain — "& yu ___ drink as
  much Ale yu can". Possibly "freely". Flag.
- "yu yu vomit drink as / more Ale" : dittography "yu yu"; "if yu vomit drink as more
  Ale". Preserved.
- "diseass" = disease. ok.

GENERAL: hand consistent; cramped in this powder recipe; many proper-noun ingredients
left near as-seen and flagged for the revision pass.
