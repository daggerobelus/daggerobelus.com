A Receipt for the Dropsey.
Take Horehound, Harts tonge, Liverworth, Worm
wood, & Sorrill, of each a large handfull shred
them very small & put them in a pasty of Barley
meale; the pye must be very well closed & baked
as other pyes are, being baked take it out, And
haveing ready a stanne of 4 Galls of new Ale
or new Strong beere Cut the pye into little
peeeces & put it into the Ale or new Beere
& even it close & let it worke, after 2 or 3
dayes drink of it as much as yu will, Cheifly a
great draught fastinge in the morninge & alsoe
at night goinge to bed; begin to drink it in
the first Quarter of the Moone, & soe drink it
till the Moone be at the full. If yu keep an
extrordinary temporate Dyet drinkinge nothinge
else it will likely cure the dropsey in 3 weekes
But to make this more perfect put into the
stanne of Ale or Beere a Quarter of an ounce
of the salt of Wormewood. //
