A Salve for sundry uses called
the Leaden plaister
XXVII
Take one pd of oyle Olive of the best & of white
Lead very well calcinated into dust, 12 ounces of
Spanish sope incorporate all these well together in
an earthen pott well glazed & when they be well incor
porated that the sope comes uppermost & put it upon
a small fire of coales, continuemge the said fire an
houre & a halfe still stirring it wth a belt of bronce
fastned to a stick of wood, yt make yr fire some
what bigger, till the redness be turned into a grey
colour but yu must not leave stirring till the
matter be turned into the colour of oyle somat
darker, then drop it on a wooden trencher & if it
neither cleave to yr fingers nor the trencher its boyled
enough then dippy cloathes in that, the sharcloth or
therein dipped will last 20 yeares, & yr older yr better
this plaister laid to the stomach awakes appetite and
takes away the paine of the stomack layd to the
belly, it is a remedy for the Collicke laid to the
reines of the back it stops the bloudy flux

Runings of the reins heat of the kidneys & weaknesse
of the back, it heales all swellings, Aches & bruises, it
breakes felllons, Impostumes, pushes & heales them
beinge applyed to the funda~t it heales all diseasses
therein laid to the head, it is good for the vrnilla

A good Cordiall for a woman in Childbed
XXVIII
Take a quarter of a pint of Cowslip water & 5 ounces of
Syrrup of Clovegillyflowers & 2 pennyworth of Cinamon
water & 5 spoonfulls of Claret wine & Mennus one
drame mingle all these together and it is a soveraigne
good cordiall for any especially for one that lyes
in Childbed
