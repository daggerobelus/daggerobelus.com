A Powder
X
Take of Corrall Amber and Iott of each one ounce, then
take 2 ounces of Pearle & 5 ounces of hartshorne burnt
with it, take the smallest rinder of the eggs & a litle
black claw of the king Crabb of each halfe an ounce, this pill
of possible must be taken when the signe is in Cancer take
a quantum ounce of the stones rb of yu shall find in the heads
of the Crawfish & take halfe an ounce of 3 bones when
dr in a stagg heart which must be burnt, halfe an ounce
of Indian Snake wood, as much of the rootes of Goats
Rhine which must be gathered in winter & well dryed
take all those ingredients & boat them into very fine
powder & grind them with redress water, upon a
painters stone & let it dry, then powder it very
fine, & scarce it through a very fine Lawne if it
will not come through boat & grind againe because yt
may not lose any of the substance, they must be
boat & scarced severally & when yr have made them
into fine powder, then weigh them againe, & take of
the Corrall Amber & Iott of equall quantities of the
Pearle & hartshorne what they yeild when they are equally
scarced then take 2 ounces of rasped hartshorne, & put
into a pipkin with 3 pints of runings water, & boyle
it till it come to a stiff Jelly & when yr think it enough
take a spoonefull & let it Coole & if it be stiff straine of
the iy, & take 4 penyworth of saffron dry it into powder and
straine it into yr Ielly whilst it is hott & soe lett it coole
then put all yr powders into an Allablaster Mortar & your
Iolly by little & little, untill it come to a past, and if you

please yr make it up into balls or into the fashion of
an eggs, or into long rovules, yr must put in the speak
wood, Goats Rhine rootes & an ounce of Coarstone, halfe
an ounce of Ambergreace & 20 graines of Musk, your
Musk & Amber greace must be powdered & put into the
Iolly

A pill for the stone in the
kidneys Collick
XI
Take Holland powder, 2 dramms Pulvis sanctus one
dram & a halfe Pulvis ad Caluceum 4 scruples, powder
mint, powder of Licoris, of each one drams, Balsamum
Indes one scruple, venice Turpentine washed very well
as much as will bringe all into a Masse to pills

Take morninge & eveninge 3 at a time when yr fitt
is about 10 times or more in a moneth, and when
the fitt is on yr take 4 or 5 pills & soe faithfull drink
as much Ale yr can possible, yr or vomit drink as
more Ale as yr can & either walk or ride 2 or 3 houres
after it and this will bring away the stone and
remove the Collick
Take some of those pills every moneth, it will
prevent the disease from cominge againe //
