A Seare Cloath for an Ache
XXI
Take halfe a pint of the best Sallett oyle, one ounce
and a halfe of white Ceros, a quarter of a pd of Adders
Lead, beat them severally, put yr oyle into a lead
pan & set it on the coales till it begin to boyle
then then put in yr Ceros Stirringe it with a long
Stick till it lookes like faire water, then put in your
leade & Stir it till it looke blackish and dropp of it
till yr can roule of it in yr hand, yr shall see it
enough boyled by droppinge it in faire water &
when it will roule yr may make yr searcloath
presently.

A Diott drink for the dropsey Short
windednesse or the Tisicke
XXII
Take one pound of Liquerish, Carreway seeds & wood
Coriander seeds, Annis seeds of each 2 ounces, parsley
one ounce unles the patient can make noe whole
than halfe an ounce will serve; Allcompaine roots
Pollipodie of each 2 ounces Bay berries 3 ounces,

Rosemary, sage, Thyme, Burrage, Hysope, Winter
savory, Pennyroyall, Woodbinde, Neath wine some
call it weatherwinde others call it Soape wine of each
one handfull, Take 3 galls of Spring water & boyle
all these together, till they come to halfe the quantity
when that is cold, Straine it through a clean
cloath, put it into earthen bottles & drinke it halfe
an houre before yr eat or drinke any thing else
in a morninge, one quarter of a pint at a draught

A receipt for a red face
XXIII
Take the Iuice of Southern wood one large
Spoone full put it into a pint of Strong Ale
or halfe a pint of Sacke drinke this by six
of the Clock in the morninge fastinge, abstaine
from eatinge or drinkinge untill Tenn a Clocke
then take Southernwood & put a little vinegar
to it & eat it with bread & butter as sage, & at
Twelve you may goe to diner, this is to be observ
ed for Tenn dayes together
