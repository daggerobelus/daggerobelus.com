To make oyle of Swallowes
VI
Take 10 or 12 younge Swallowes ready to fly stamp them
them in a stone Mortar with their quills & feathers togeth
er Lavender, Cotten, Balme, Spearmint, sedauge, Camemill
wild strawberries leaves & rose thyme of each a handfull take
let these heartly the one day unwashed after they be gathered
then stamp all together with the swallows till noe feathers
be left whole, put thereto a good quantity of May butter
or fresh butter & let all stand in the Mortar a while then put it in the
Mortar, the next day boyle all those very softly three quarters
of an houre or a whole house, then straine it through a
cleane Linen Cloath, into a Cleane vessell & keepe it
for your uses.

To make a Jelly of Hartshorne
VII
Take a pint & a halfe of faire runing water & halfe a
pint of white wine, two ounces of hartshorde scraped let it
boyle softly in a pipkin close covered till it come to a pint, then make
it is hott straine it & put to it a litle sliced ginger & Cynament stick
the next day take of 2 topp of a leave & to thone of it if yu may
have & claret & put it in a skellett & put to it suggar a topp of
sweet Marjeram, halfe a spoonefull of see roes water, a little juice of a
Lemon & an Orange, see let it stand a litle while upon yr fire & put
through a jelly bagg in a litle rosemary springe in 4 bottoms of yr bagg
take it morning & evening 4 or 3 times in a day & cole or
else melt it, soe drink it bloud warme //

For the dropsy, yellow Iaundice, itches, breakings
out & Mangies of yr body to putrifye yr blood, from corrupt
ag, & green sicknes & all stoppinge, very good for young
maids                              & Calenu &c.
VIII
Take the rootes of Monkes Rubarbe otherwise called patience
& seed Madder of each halfe a pd, 4 ounces, of chissvedes and
Lickoris of each 2 ounces, Scabiens & Egrimony of each one
handfull, slice the rootes of Rubarb, scrape & chisseds and
Lickoris boake & hearke with yr hand, put them into a stone
pott called a stand with 3 galls of strong Ale, to steepe
the space of three dayes then drink this as your orena
ry liquor for the space of 3 weekes at the least, the longer
you take it the better provideo you have one stand
under another //

To cure any Fistula though of 20 yeares old
IX
Take of roch Allom, & white Copperos of each alike quan
tity boyle them in an Earthen pot over a soft fire, when it
is hard then breake it in an Iron mortar, sift it as oft times
through a launes, put as much of this powder as will lye on a
shillinge in a quart of runing water, see sallinge, & wash the
wound or sore therewith twice a day, lay on the Lent or
roule in the same water upon yr wound, & lay this poultesse as
followeth upon the cloathes that it may touch the sore
Take Sontron, ground Ivy, smallage, dassy roots, & leaves,
Mallowes of each a handfull, chop them very small, & boyle them
in a quart of runing water with a quarter of a pd of
mutton suett, when the boils are tender thicken it wth wheat
branne, lay a fresh poultess every morninge. &c. //
