To Pickle Walnuts
Take yr Walnuts at ye bignesse that yu may run a pin
through & put them in a pot & cover them wth a pickle
made of salt & water, boyle the pickle up with wine
leaves, lett them lye a weeke or a fortnight in the
pickle, if you like it, if they mould put a peece of
rockd allome in & cover them close; then take to a
pint of whitewine a quart of whitewine vinegar
& boyle yr with salt & lett the pickle be cold, then
take the nutts out of the pickle & cross them with
a knife and scrape the inwards out with a sliver
and fill them with mustard, pepper, Ginger, and
garlick mixt all together, Lay them close together
in the pott & power the pickle to them, Cover them
with a Slate or Tile //

For the risinge of the Lytes
XXX
Take red rose water a quarter of a pint or alsoe a
peny worth of Saffron tyed up in a Lawne bagg or
Tiffany steep it in water a while then crush it betweene
two spoones till yu make the water an Amber Collour

then give two spoonefuls of it, to the partie when the fitt
cometh & if it doth not Lay it give two spoonefulls more
immediately for three or four sundry fitts

A Receipt for the Wormes
or a very good Purge
XXXI
Take 4 peny worth of Alloes & 2 peny worth of worme
seed & 2 peny worth of hartshorne it must be powder
of hartshorne & one peny worth of Curralline, bruise
the alloes & pound the wormes seed very small & pownd
the Curralline to powder then mingle them together
give it 3 morninges together as much as will lye on a
six pence in a spoonefull of Beere & then wash your
mouth wth an other spoonefull of Beere & fast 2 houres
after it, & then take a little thin broth & keep yr selfe
warme, be sure yr doe not get cold

A very good Medicine for any heate in the face
or other sore, although it be St Anthony's fire
XXXII
Take a pottle of smiths water a handfull of sage, 2 hand
fulls of Elder leaves or the ground Ivies of it & 2 dittom seeth
all these from a pottle to a pint, put it in an earthen or galy pott
& lett yr patient annoynt his face wth it when he goeth to bed in this
next morninge he shall find ease, but in 5 or 6 dayes (by
Gods grace) helpe.
