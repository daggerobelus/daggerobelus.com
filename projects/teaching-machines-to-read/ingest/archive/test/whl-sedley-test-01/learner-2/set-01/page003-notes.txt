PAGE 003 — "A Receipt for the Dropsey" (original foliation "1", top right)

Generally clear italic/round hand. Flags:

L2 "Liverworth" — reads Liver + worth (terminal -worth, doubled like other
   herb names; cf. "Mugworth" later pages). Letterforms clear; flag only the
   final cluster -worth vs -wort. [Liverworth?] but I read worth.
L2 "tonge" — "Harts tonge" (hartstongue fern). o-n-g-e clear; scribe's spelling.
L4 "pasty" — p-a-s-t-y, long-s; reads "pasty" (a pasty/pie casing). clear.
L7 "stanne" — s-t-a-n-n-e, doubled n; appears again L18. Reading "stanne"
   (a vessel/stand? possibly "stanne"=tin vessel). Letterforms clear though
   word unusual; preserved as written. [stanne?] — letterforms support it.
L7 "Galls" — "4 Galls" = gallons (measure). G-a-l-l-s. clear.
L9 "peeeces" — appears to have an extra e: p-e-e-e-c-e-s. Possible dittographic
   minim. Transcribed as seen ("peeeces"); flag [peeeces?] minim-count uncertain
   — could be "peeces" (2 e's). Preserved triple as the safer literal reading.
L10 "yu" = you (superscript u over y), lowered per convention. recurs L14.
L11 "fastinge","morninge" — terminal -inge with -e; scribe habit. clear.
L15 "extrordinary" — e-x-t-r-o-r-d-i-n-a-r-y; scribe omits first 'a'
    (ext[r]ordinary). Preserved as written (omitted letter, not corrected).
L15 "temporate" — t-e-m-p-o-r-a-t-e (for temperate); preserved.
L16 "else" — first word; long-s; "elſe". clear.
L19/last "Probatum est" — recipe-end formula, set lower right. clear.

ORTHOGRAPHY confirmed: -inge endings, doubled ll (Sorrill, Galls, full),
ye/yu abbreviations, & for and.
