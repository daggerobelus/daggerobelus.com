To make Snayle water
Take a good peck of garden snayles in their shells
& wash them in a great bowle of beere, then make
your Chimney hearth very cleane & pour out half
a bushell of Charcoales and set them on fire, when
they are throughly kindled, then with a fire shovell
make a great hole in the midst of them & pour the
snayles & scatter some fire of yr quick coales amongst
them, & see let them rest till they have done makeing
a noise, And then you must take them forth and
with a knife and course cloth pick & wipe them
from the ashes & coles & the greene froth that is upon
them. Then in a stone Mortar bruise them shells &
all, take alsoe a quart of earth wormes, & secure them
with water very well divers times over, then wash them
with pure white salt, then put them into a stone Mortar
& beat them well to peeces, then take a cleane Iron pot you
have wch fott yr Limbeck or still, then take 2 handfulls
of Sallendine & lay in the bottome of yr pott & 2 hand
fulls of Angelica must be laide upon that, then put
in a Quart or two of rosmary flowers alsoe boarsel
Agrimony Reddock roots, Barke of Barbary trees, wood
Sorrill, Buttony, & sack of them two handfulls, half
a handfull of Rew & of fenegrick & Turnerick of sack
an ounce well beaten, then lay yr snayles & wormes
upon the top of all those hearts and flowers

Then pour in three galls of the strongest Ale then Cover
your pott & lett it stand all night & longer if yr please
in the place where yu meane to make yr fire under it.
In the morninge yu must put in three ounces of Cloves
beaten to powder, & peny waight of saffron drye to powder
Harts horne fower or five ounces, yu must not stirr it after
yu have put in the Hartshorne for it must be approved
then sett your Limbeck & Close it with Rye dough & make
a very fast fire under it & receive yr water by pints till
the first is strongest the next weaker & weaker, but the weakest
is usefull, the smallest if need be may be helped with putny
in one spoonefull of the strongest to 4 of the smallest, It is
to be use two spoonefulls of the strongest to 4 of else or Beere
or with wine, of the snayles as much of Ale or Bee or of
wine, This water is by experience good for the yellow
Iaundise & at all griefes cominge from the obstructions
of the Liver helps the Consumption, it must be taken in
the morning two houres before made in the after
noone with some moderate stirrings of the body
after the takeinge of it &c

Aqua Mirabilis
Take of the seedes of Cardimum, Cubebbs, Gallingall
Mellelot flowers, Cloves, Mace, Nutmegs ginger of each
one dramme, beinge made into powder putt them into
3 pints of white wine & one pint of aqua vita, a pint of
the Iuice of Sallendine putt all these into a glasse Still and
soe lett them incorporate all night, in the morning distill it
