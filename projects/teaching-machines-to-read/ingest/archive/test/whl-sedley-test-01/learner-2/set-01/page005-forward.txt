Palsy Water
Take of Borrage flowers, Bugles flowers, the flowers
of Lillys Convaly, Cowslips of each one handfull,
of Sage flowers & Rosmary flowers & Lidiany flowers
of each halfe a handfull let all those be gathered in their
severall seasons for they are not to be had all at one
time, but take the first that cometh & let them be steep
ed in very good spirit of wine distilled from ash
phlegme in fine glasses till all may be had, And let them
stand some dayes after the last is in, but always very close
stopped, Else when Lavender cometh stripp of the fairest
heads the quantity of 2 & put them into a large glass
glass & pour on them as much spirit of wine as before
soe Cover them well stopped & let them stand 14 dayes
