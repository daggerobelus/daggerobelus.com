=== REVISIONS / EXTENSIONS TO LETTERFORMS ===

a — confirmed single-compartment round a. In numbered-list contexts the scribe
    also writes a flat-topped a (e.g. "Ague", "Aloes"). No new confusion.

b — confirmed. Distinguished from h by absence of below-line descender. The
    "boat"(=beat) token recurs (pp.008-009): b + o-shaped a + t. The scribe
    genuinely writes the vowel as 'oa' here — habitual, not error-once.

d — italic straight d more common than looped secretary d in this hand. d-loop
    can be misread as the tail giving false "-ar"/"-er" (cf. set-01 "seared"→
    "seed"). Watch d-final.

e — confirmed reversed-loop e. Terminal -e nearly universal. The "ee" in
    "beere","keep","Sheepe","weeke" is two clear loops.

g — looped descender; in "-inge" endings the g is small + e: "morninge",
    "eveninge","cominge","stoppinge". Very regular habit.

r — the 2-shaped italic r dominates mid-word; a long r appears word-initial
    and after o ("rootes","Roses"). The hooked terminal -r graph appears in
    "powder","water","Mortar".

s — long-s initial/medial; round terminal s. Doubled long-s "ſſ" in "glasse",
    "Masse","disolve"(single). Note "fſ"/"ſſ" cluster easy to overcount.

t — short crossed t; in "-tt" finals ("fitt","pott","Iott") two short uprights.

I/J — capital I/J used for J: "Iott"(jet), "Iuice","Iaundice","Ielly","Iolly",
    "Ioynts". Always transcribed as written (I).

numbers — Roman numerals head each recipe (VI–XXIII) centered under the title.
    Arabic numerals appear inline for quantities ("2 ounces","3 galls","20
    graines"). The form "yr"/"yu" remain superscript abbreviations lowered.

=== NEW / CONFIRMED ABBREVIATIONS & GRAPHS ===
pd  = pound (lb symbol), KEPT as-is per Folger money/measure exception.
wch = which ; wth = with ; yt = that ; yu = you ; yr = your.
ye  = the (thorn-y), e.g. "for ye Palsy","ye Tooth Ache","ye Rosemary".
&c. = etc. ; & = and (looped ampersand, confirmed — resolved set-01 "& inw").
Probatum / Probatum est = recipe-end attestation.
"thone" = the one (the-elision), seen p.007.

=== ORTHOGRAPHIC HABITS (confirmed/added) ===
- "boat" for beat; "see"/"soe" both used for so; "power" for pour.
- doubled consonants: "skillet/skellett","pott","fitt","Iott","stiffe","glasse".
- "-inge" verbal endings standard. terminal -e standard.
- spice/herb proper names highly idiosyncratic and often the hardest tokens
  (chissveds, Sontron, Bauterries, Cloworthes, Goats Rhine) — these are the
  recurring flag sources, NOT general-letter problems.
- Latin drug names appear (Pulvis sanctus, Pulvis ad Calc-, Balsamum) — read
  letter-by-letter, flag where the Latin is uncertain.

=== CONFUSION-RISK UPDATE ===
- d-final vs -ar/-er tail (overcorrect risk; cf seed/seared).
- "boat"/"boyle" both begin bo- ; check the t vs yl.
- ingredient names: do NOT normalize to known herbs unless strokes resolve.
- minim miscounts in long-s clusters and in "-nn-"/"-mm-" (flannell, stanne).
