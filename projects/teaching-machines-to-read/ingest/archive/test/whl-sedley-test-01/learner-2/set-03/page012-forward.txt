Aqua preservative against the plague
or pestilence
XXIV
Take Sage of vertue Rue otherwise called herbe
grace, Elder leaves & red bramble leaves of each
of them a good handfull stamp them together
& then straine them through a fine Linnen cloth
and put to the Iuice a quart of good whitewine
and a good quantity of whitewine vinegar
mingle them together & put hereto a quarter
of an ounce of white Ginger beaten to small
powder & soe to drink this medicine every morn
inge fastinge for the space of 9 or 10 dayes
together the quantity of a spoonefull at a time
and this will (say God) helpe & preserve yr for ye
space of a whole yeare

A Drink for the Ricketts
XXV
Take of Bettany Egrimony, Hartstonge, Liver
worte, Colts foot & Scarious shuggar of each halfe a
handfull, the middle rhine of ash, & dens rootes
of each a quarter of an ounce, Water cresses

Break wind of each one handfull, fliggs sliced 30, Raisins
of the sun stoned 3 ounces, boyle all these in a pottle of
faire water unto a quart & when it is almost boyled
put in a quarter of an ounce of Liquorish straine it &
lett her drink of it 3 or 4 times a day & alsoe in the
night of she 18th than to drinke you must put in
hartshorne rasped halfe an ounce

A Medicine for Freckles
XXVI
Take a quart of white wine & a pottle of the flowers
of Rosemary put them in a good glass bottle, then fill a
great skillett with water & sett it in hay & soe lett
it in the skillett before yu sett it on the fire, & soe lett
it grow very warme but not boyle at all, then take a pint
of white wine vinegar, put a new laid egg in it and let
it remaine in the vinegar till the egg be consumed
or disolved then straine the vinegar & whitewine & soe
soe put the liquor in a clean glass, then give it
a warme over the fire & soe keep it for yr use
Take a pint of Plantin water & a pint of femitory
water, drinke a quarter of a pint every morninge &
and fast an houre after it
1686
