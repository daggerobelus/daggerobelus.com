XXXVII
Take the Root of double Crowfoot that beareth a double
yellow flower glistering like Gold, or instead thereof a
Clave of Garlick stamp it in a wooden dish, and fill
halfe a Nasel nuttshell full of the same, and tye the same
in the inn part neat to the Lower side of the left Arme
& handfull from the Wrest, and let it remain there about
six houres, then, open the same, and you shall find a
litle Bladder in the same place, if it be not often enough
in the said 12 houres, let it remain untill the time that
the said Bladder be risen, & cut Out of the skin thereof
& the said Bladder be fallen, & it be not fallen prick it
with a pin, then let it & & and then take a peice of the
branch of an Elder tree of the greenest as bigg as a peys
pare of the bark, and tye the end thereof to the place
aforesaid, and three Ivy leaves uppon the same, first
prickt full of holes, and let it remain there soe long
untill it have made a hole that will receive a peys, then
put therein a peys & and lay thereon iij Ivie leaves
prickt, and soe shift the same with a peys, and three Ivie
leaves morning and evening and keep the same Plast
and every if dayes, as you shall soe need, wash your

Arme with French Wines blood warme, and do continue
with the same untill the danger of the Pestilence
be past
The Order to heale the Same
XXXVIII
Fourteen dayes before you will heale the Same, you must
beware of Surfetting, and use noe person nor Beanes, and
take halfe a pint of French wine and halfe a pint of Sallett
oyle, and mingle them together, and bath the Sore therewith
twice or trice aday, and tye a Cloth wet therewith to the
Same, and Soe it will be well Healed.

To make the Botch appear
XXXIX
Take a handfull of Sorrill otherwise cald greene sawce
stamp, and straine it with a draught of Beere or Ale,
take a litle Treacle and the powder of one Nutmegg,
mixe em all together, give it the sick to drink and the
Botch will appear within five houres after
probatum est

To break a Botch or Boyle
XL
Take Vnlimed Egrimony and Snailes, stamp em
together and Lay it to the sore
Another for the same
XLI
