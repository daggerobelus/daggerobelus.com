A Receipt for the cure of an Ague
XII
Take Elder Stickes of one yeares shoot newly cut from the tree
scrape of the upper bark first which is naught & then scrape of
the green inward barke & of that take a good handfull & bruise
in a stone Mortar & weeven boorle then cut it into a pint of stronge
Ale and let it infuse all night before the fire, the pott be covered
soe that it may be warme and not more and a litle before the
partye expects the fitt straine the Ale from the barke, the last
beinge squeesed is the strongest and best, & put into it better than
halfe a spoonefull of pepper grosely beaten, and see soone as they
finde any grudginge of the fitt drink it and stirre up and
downe, and in twice takeing it will Cure
It hath been very often proved

To make Pills for the head & Stomach
XIII
Take an ounce of Aloes, a quarter of an ounce of Rubarb; halfe
a quarter of an ounce of Cinnamum Resitum, beat these into
fine powder & make these into pills mingled with the
Compound of Syrrup of wormewood, take three of them
halfe an houre before Supper, and see Continue every third
night till the whole Masse be taken
Probatum
After the pills have wrought drink some possett drinke
about two hours after.

A Drink Good for ye Palsy
XIV
Take of the shavings of the Stalks of ye Rosemary halfe a x
weight Orange peels, sliced braine of each one handfull, yt
slags dryed by the fire & lay it 100 24 handfulls, put these in 2 or
3 galls of new shoote into a bottinge bagg & hang it in a Vessell
of 4 or 5 gallons of Ale or beere of a midele strength & drink it
noe other drinke for a whole yeare

Pils for the head
XV
Take of Aloes & dram the rootes of wild gernes of all sorts of
Mirabilans Diagedeum, Mastiche Bauterries, Roses of each
halfe a drame of saffron a scruple stamp them well together
with the Iuice of Cloworthes & take saven pils of the same
Morninge and Eveninge

A Medicine for the stone
XVI
Take the seeds of Saxefrige rootes dryed to powder three
times in a weeke, every day as much of the powder as
will lye upon a six pence in white wine, the next weeke
drink it twice in the weeke, the third weeke once,
see this every Quarter of the yeare
