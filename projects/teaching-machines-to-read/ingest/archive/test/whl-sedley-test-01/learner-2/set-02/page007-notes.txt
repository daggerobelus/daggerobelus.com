PAGE 007 — LEFT (fol.8): "To make oyle of Swallowes" (VI) + "To make a
Jelly of Hartshorne" (VII); RIGHT (fol.9): dropsy/jaundice recipe (VIII) +
"To cure any Fistula" (IX).

LEFT
- title "To make oyle of Swallowes" clear; recipe number VI (roman, centered).
- "stamp them them" L1-2 — dittography across line break ("stamp them /
  them in a stone Mortar"); preserved as written.
- "sedauge" — herb in list (Lavender, Cotten, Balme, Spearmint, sedauge,
  Camemill). reads s-e-d-a-u-g-e; uncertain. [sedauge?] possibly "sedge"/
  "sea-age". flag.
- "Camemill" — camomile; clear-ish.
- "take let these heartly the one day unwashed" — "heartly" uncertain;
  reads h-e-a-r-t-l-y. [heartly?] possibly "hartly"/"lye". flag the clause —
  "let these heartly" odd. flag.
- "a whole house" — "an houre or a whole house" — clearly should be "hour(e)"
  repeated; reads h-o-u-s-e. scribe slip. [house?] kept literal. flag.
- VII "Jelly of Hartshorne": "hartshorde" L2 — h-a-r-t-s-h-o-r-d-e; scribe
  wrote -horde for -horne (d/n slip). preserved literal. [hartshorde?] flag.
- "2 topp of a leave & to thone of it" — muddy clause. reads "topp of a
  leave"; "thone"=the one (the-elision). [leave?/thone?] flag whole clause.
- "skellett" — skillet; clear-ish.
- "see roes water" — "halfe a spoonefull of see roes water" — "see roes"
  uncertain; possibly "rose water". [see roes?] flag. recurs pattern below.
- "springe in 4 bottoms" — "rosemary springe in 4 bottoms of yr bagg" —
  "bottoms" b-o-t-t-o-m-s; uncertain (bottoms? buttons?). [bottoms?] flag.

RIGHT
- VIII heading is a long descriptive rubric, not a "To make" title. Clear-ish.
  "putrifye yr blood from corrupt ag, & green sicknes" — "corrupt ag" cut /
  abbreviation? reads c-o-r-r-u-p-t a-g; [corrupt ag?] possibly "corruption".
  flag. Marginal "& Calenu &c." top-right uncertain. [Calenu?] flag.
- "Monkes Rubarbe otherwise called patience" — monk's rhubarb (patience dock).
  clear-ish.
- "chissvedes"/"chisseds" — recurring odd word "of chissvedes and Lickoris";
  later "scrape & chisseds". reads c-h-i-s-s-...; [chissveds?] uncertain
  ingredient. flag — revisit.
- "Lickoris" — licorice; scribe spelling. clear.
- "boake & hearke with yr hand" — "Lickoris boake & hearke" — very uncertain;
  reads b-o-a-k-e & h-e-a-r-k-e. [boake/hearke?] possibly "breake & shred".
  flag whole clause, high priority.
- "a stand" / "one stand under another" — vessel called "a stand"; recurs.
  clear-ish.
- "orenary liquor" — "your orenary liquor" = ordinary; o-r-e-n-a-r-y, scribe
  spelling. [orenary?] clear-ish.
- "provideo" — "the better provideo you have" = provided; p-r-o-v-i-d-e-o,
  scribe slip (o for d). [provideo?] kept literal. flag.

IX Fistula
- "roch Allom" — rock alum. "white Copperos"=copperas (white vitriol). clear.
- "launes" — "sift it as oft times through a launes" = lawn (fine cloth);
  l-a-u-n-e-s. [launes?] clear-ish.
- "see sallinge" — "in a quart of runing water, see sallinge" — uncertain;
  "see"=soe? "sallinge"=salting? reads s-a-l-l-i-n-g-e. [see sallinge?] flag.
- "the Lent or roule" — "lay on the Lent or roule in the same water" — "Lent"
  =lint; "roule"=roll. l-e-n-t / r-o-u-l-e. clear-ish.
- "Sontron" — herb "Take Sontron, ground Ivy, smallage" — reads S-o-n-t-r-o-n;
  uncertain. [Sontron?] possibly "Centron"/"Scordium". flag.
- "mutton suett" — mutton suet. "wheat branne" — wheat bran. clear.

Numerous flags; right-column ingredient names (chissveds, boake/hearke,
Sontron, Calenu) are the priority revisits.
