The order of drawinge your aquacomposita:

Halfe your sinamun,  your lignum allowes, the Rootes & seedes of Angelica must be reserved
out of the brasse pott & knitt in a linen bagge, & layd into the receyvor, wher
your lycour must Runne into, & by & by your Alkauitt must be putt into the receyver
also reserve out of the brasse pott all your herbes & flowers vntill the last draught
comme; All your other spices, seedes, rootes & gommes must be putt with halfe the Ale
in the brasse pott, then sett your lymbecke & close it fast, keapinge the head of
your limbecke alwayes with could water, & drawinge it so longe, as it will come
good which is tryed by castinge alittle of it into the fier, yf it burne it is good
or els nott, then take of your limbecke charely, & power out all the stuffe that
is in the little bagges that is in the Receyver, & all the rest of your flowere &
herbes & a gallon & a halfe of freshe Ale (beinge the other halfe) putt vnto
them, & sett on your limbecke, & drawe it as you did before, & the sinamun &
sugar was lefte must be putt in the Receyvor agayne, when all is drawen out,
putt your first draught & last together & keepe it for your vse:

howe to make a perfume to preserve mankind from the Infection
of the plauge, & also to bourne it in presses for the
preservation of Clothes, beddinges or hanginges 2.

Take puer ould giniper, shave it as thinne as maye be an vnce of the flower & herbe
of Saint Iohns worte halfe an vnce, of the Gummes of olibanum, of masticke, of puer
Chosen mirre, of eche of thos one vnce, your gummes grinde greate, your saint Iohns worte
hacke smale your shavinges of giniper in lyke sorte , then mixte all together; & putt
them in some convenient vessell, or boxe, then add therto puer turpentine of venishe as
muche as will make it lyke a peece of paste, take the quantitiye of halfe a nvttmegg, &
laye it on a hott tyle eyther in your Chamber or in the parlor beneathe when you
goe to bed at nighte./

The order of makinge three kindes of pepper
for expellinge of Wynde:  3

Take of puer suger fower vnces, Rose water as muche as will moysten your suger which is
in the posenett, then haue redye thes spices followinge, White pepper, black pepper, of
eche of thes halfe a dramme, puer gallingale sliced, & fine minced, ginger pared & fine minced
of eche of thos one scrupell & a halfe, puer Chosen curbitt white & goummey cleane scraped
thinne slyced & finelye minced two scrupells, your Rose water & suger scumme it as cleane
as may be & then lett them boyle with a softe fire, then putt in all your spices aforesaid
& sturre them diligentlye, then take it from the fier, & sturr it till it be could &
thicke, & thes putt into a glasse pott & reserve to your vse, of this you may take the
quantitye of a peny or two pence at a tyme: -
