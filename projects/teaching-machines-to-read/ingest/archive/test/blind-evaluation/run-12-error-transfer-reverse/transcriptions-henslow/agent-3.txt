My lorde of Caunterberries
Brothe for a Consumptione
Take like as before sauinge put thereto a handfull of violett leaues
langdebeefe leaues Borrage leaues and Strawberrie leaues, knit
togeather of euerie one a like quantitye.

For the pyles
Take one handefull of mollet leaues, and wrappe them in a Coleworte
leafe, and bake them in the [imbers?] untill they bee well rosted
then take them, and put awaye the Coleworte leafe, and grinde
them in a morter, and put to yt oyle of roses, and grinde them togeather
then take it and laye it to the pyles withe a [trosser?], and vse this
morninge and euenynge.

For the flixe
Take horsedonge new made and frie it withe sweete butter
and put it in a cloathe and laye it to the fundament as hott
as you maye suffer ytt, and lye vppon vntill you haue ease.

For Deafnes
Take blacke woole whiche growes vppon the flanke
of a sheepe and tarre, and mingle them togeather
and put them into the eare.
