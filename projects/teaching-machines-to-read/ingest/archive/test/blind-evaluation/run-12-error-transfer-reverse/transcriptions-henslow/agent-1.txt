My lorde of Caunterberries
Brothe for a Consumptione

Take like as before savinge put thereto a handfull of violett leaves
Langdebeefe leaves Boorrage leaves and Strawberrie leaves, knitt
togeather of euerie one alike quantitye.

for the pyles

Take one handefull of mollett leaves, and wrappe them in a Colewort
leafe, and rake them in the [imbers?] untill they bee well rosted
then take them, and put awaye the Coleworte leafe, and grinde
them in a morter, and put to yt oyle of roses, and grinde them togeather
then take it and laye it to the pyles withe a trussie, and vse this
morninge and eveninge.

for the flixe

Take horselounge new made and frie it withe sweete butter
and put it in a Clothe and laye it to the fundament, as hott
as you maye suffer yt, and lye vppon untill you haue ease.

for Deafnes

Take blacke wolle whiche growes vppon the flanke
of a sheepe and [hare?], and mingell them togeather
and put them into the eare
