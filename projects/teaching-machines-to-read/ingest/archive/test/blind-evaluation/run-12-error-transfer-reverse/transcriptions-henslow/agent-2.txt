My lorde of Caunterberries
Brothe for a Consumptione

Take like as before sauinge putt thereto a handefull of violett leaues
langdebeefe leaues Borrage leaues and Strawberrie leaues, knitt
together of euerie one a like quantitye.

For the Pyles

Take one handefull of mollett leaues, and wrappe them in a Colewortt
leafe, and [bake?] them in the Imbers untill they bee well rested
then take them, and putt awaye the Colewortt leafe, and grinde
them in a morter, and putt to yt oyle of roses, and grinde them together
then take it and laye it to the pyles withe a trosser, and vse this
morninge and euenynge.

For the flixe

Take horsedounge new made and frie itt withe sweete butter
and putt it in a Cloathe and laye itt to the fundament as hott
as you maye suffer ytt, and lye vppon untill you haue ease.

For Deafnes

Take blacke wolle whiche growes vppon the flanke
of a sheepe and Tarre and mingell them togeather
and putt them into the eare
