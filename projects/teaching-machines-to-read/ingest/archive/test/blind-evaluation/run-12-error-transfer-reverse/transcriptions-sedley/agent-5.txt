A Salve for sundry vses called
the Leaden plaister
XXVII

Take one pd of oyle oliue of the best & of red
lead very well searced with dust 12 ounces of
Spanish sope incorporate all these well together in
an earthen pot well glazed & when the collour is
turned that the sope comes aboue sett it vpon
a small fire of coales continuinge the said fire an
houre & a halfe still stirringe it on a bole of browne
fastend to a sticke of wood, yt make it fire some
what eager, till the reddnes be turned into a grey
colour but yt must not leaue stirringe till the
matter be turned into the coulour of oyle colour
darker, then drop it on a wooden trencher & if it
neither cleaue to yt fingers nor the trencher favoureth
enough, then dripp it thicker in that, the thicker you
thereon dipped will last 20 yeares, p yt glory yt haues
this plaister laid to the stomach Comforts appetites and
takes away the paines of the Stomacke laid to the
reines of the backe it stayes the bloudy fluxe

[Page 21]

Openings of the reines [heat?] of the Kidneys or weaknes
of the backe, it heales all swellings, Aches & [bruises?], at
[frankes?] ffellons, Impostumes, pushes & styles them &
[brings?] applyed to the forehead it heales all diseases
thereon laid to the head, it is good for the [ruinest?]

A Tried Cordiall for Mr Nowill in Childbed
XXVIII

Take a quarter of a pinte of Cowslip water & 2 ounces of
Syropp of Cleare [Gilliflowers?] & a pennyworth of Cinnamon
water & a Spoonefull of Clarett wine & [Manus?]
[Christi?] mingle all these together and it is a soueranu
good cordiall for any especially for one that lyes
in Childbed.

Pills for the [Gall?]
XXIX

[...] two [...] graines and [...]
ffor A Tetter

Take one Handfull of Sallandine & as much bay
salt as can take vp betweene yt finger & Thumbe &
the iuice of halfe a Lemon stamp yt together in a
Mortar & straine the iuice out & smale the places and
yt put some of the hearbs vpon it, vse this 2 or 3
dayes together dressinge it 3 or 4 times in a Day
together &c.
