Hand-Specific Alphabet: Sedley MS534, Page 13
================================================

This opening shows two pages with what appears to be at least two different hands.
The left page (verso, numbered 20) contains recipe XXVII in a relatively neat italic-influenced hand.
The right page (recto) contains recipes XXVIII and XXIX in a similar but slightly different hand.
The script is smaller and more closely spaced than Henslow.

MINUSCULES (lowercase)
----------------------
a - Open form, sometimes resembles modern a; seen in "Salve", "all", "water"
b - Standard ascender with bowl; seen in "best", "but", "body"
c - Round form; seen in "colour", "cease", "cordial"
d - Secretary d with leftward looping ascender; seen in "and", "dust", "drop"
e - Secretary e (reversed loop) but also italic e forms; seen in "the", "ore", "ever"
f - Crossbar through ascender; seen in "for", "fire", "first"
g - Descending tail; seen in "good", "great"
h - Descender sometimes loops below; seen in "the", "that", "this"
i - Simple minim with dot; seen in "in", "it", "is"
k - Looped form; seen in "Take"
l - Simple ascender; seen in "all", "lead", "last"
m - Three minims; seen in "must", "make"
n - Two minims; seen in "not", "new", "and"
o - Round; seen in "of", "one", "ore"
p - Standard descender; seen in "put", "plaster", "pint"
q - With descender; seen in "quarter"
r - Multiple forms including round r; seen in "ore", "for", "stir"
s - Long-s medially, round s finally; seen in "Salve", "sores", "eyes"
t - Short vertical with crossbar; seen in "the", "take", "that"
u/v - v initially, u medially; seen in "very", "upon", "use"
w - Double-v form; seen in "well", "with", "water"
x - Crossed; seen in possible "mixe"
y - Descender with dot or tail; seen in "you", "your"
z - Not clearly visible

MAJUSCULES (uppercase)
----------------------
A - Elaborate form; seen in recipe title
S - Decorative; seen in "Salve"
T - Large with crossbar; seen in "Take"
C - Curved; seen in possible capitals
L - Ascender form; seen in "Leaden"

ABBREVIATIONS AND SPECIAL MARKS
---------------------------------
- Superscript letters visible in abbreviated words
- "wth" likely present (with)
- Thorn may appear as y-form for "the"
- Recipe numbering: Roman numerals XXVII, XXVIII, XXIX
- The -er graph visible in some words

CONFUSABLE LETTERS IN THIS HAND
---------------------------------
- e / o: Secretary e vs round o - need to check loop direction
- c / r / t: Can be similar in this smaller hand
- long-s / f: Must check for crossbar
- m / n / u: Minim confusion likely in this cramped hand
- The smaller script makes some letters harder to distinguish
