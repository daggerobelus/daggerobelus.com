Confidence Notes
================
This is one of the most challenging manuscripts in the set. Both pages contain
small, cramped handwriting that is difficult to read at this resolution.

Major difficulties:
- LEFT PAGE: The bottom third is severely affected by water/brown staining
  that renders much of the text illegible.
- Both pages have very small script that makes individual letterforms
  hard to distinguish.
- Many words on both pages are marked as uncertain because the letterforms
  cannot be clearly traced.
- The right page contains several recipes but the small size and somewhat
  faded ink make many readings tentative.

The transcription contains a high proportion of uncertain readings ([?] flags).
Many words marked with [?] are best guesses based on partial letterforms and
should be treated with caution. Words marked with [...] indicate passages
where letterforms could not be distinguished at all.

Specific areas of concern:
- Left page, lines 5-10: Very difficult to read; many uncertain readings
- Left page, bottom: Water damage renders text illegible
- Right page, recipe texts: Small script with many uncertain readings
- The overall density of uncertain readings on this page suggests that
  a higher-resolution image would significantly improve transcription accuracy.
