Hand-Specific Alphabet: Henslow MS688, Page 12
================================================

This is a clear, formal secretary hand with decorative elements.
The script is well-spaced and relatively easy to read.

MINUSCULES (lowercase)
----------------------
a - Open-topped form, similar to modern a; seen in "Take", "handfull", "leaves"
b - Standard form with ascender loop; seen in "Brothe", "before", "Borrages"
c - Round form, sometimes resembles secretary e; seen in "Caunteberries", "Colewort"
d - Secretary d with looped ascender curving left; seen in "lorde", "handfull"
e - Secretary e (reversed loop form); seen in "the", "leaves", "berries" - distinct from c by its narrower, more angular shape
f - Standard with crossbar through ascender; seen in "for", "fflixt" (double-f = capital F); distinct from long-s
g - Open-tailed g descending below line; seen in "Borrages", "grinde"
h - Descender loops below the line; seen in "the", "them", "handfull"
i - Simple minim with dot; seen in "in", "it", "violett"
k - Elaborate form with loops; seen in "Take" (prominent in recipe openings)
l - Simple ascender stroke; seen in "leaves", "lorde", "handfull"
m - Three minims; seen in "them", "morter"
n - Two minims; seen in "one", "in", "and"
o - Round form; seen in "of", "one", "Colewort"
p - Standard descender; seen in "put", "pyles"; watch for "special" p
q - Descender curves right; seen in "quantitye"
r - Multiple forms: round r after o, standard r elsewhere; seen in "lorde", "berries"
s - Long-s (ſ) in initial/medial position (like f without full crossbar); round s at word-end; seen in "Strawberrie", "leaves"
t - Short vertical with crossbar; seen in "the", "take", "put"
u/v - v at word start ("vpon", "violett"), u in middle ("put", "untill"); interchangeable
w - Double-v form with rounded bottom; seen in "wrappe", "with"
x - Crossed form; seen in "fflixe"
y - Descender with dot; seen in "pyles", "they"
z - Not clearly visible on this page

MAJUSCULES (uppercase)
----------------------
B - Elaborate looped form; seen in "Brothe", "Borrages"
C - Large curved form; seen in "Caunteberries", "Colewort", "Consumptione"
D - Large looped form; seen in "Deftues" (at bottom)
F - Written as ff (double-f); seen in "ffor", "fflixe"
M - Elaborate form with initial downstroke; seen in "My"
S - Large decorative form; seen in "Strawberrie"
T - Large form with horizontal crossbar; seen in "Take" (very prominent, decorated)

ABBREVIATIONS AND SPECIAL MARKS
---------------------------------
- Thorn (y-like = "th"): visible in "the" forms throughout
- -es graph: looped downstroke at word endings
- Long-s vs f: long-s lacks the full crossbar that f has
- Superscript letters present in some words
- Decorative dot patterns (. . . . .) used as line fillers between recipe titles

CONFUSABLE LETTERS IN THIS HAND
---------------------------------
- e / c / o: The secretary e is reversed-loop; must distinguish from round c and o
- long-s / f: Long-s lacks crossbar; f has crossbar
- h / b: h descender loops below; b has different ascender curve
- m / n / u: Minim confusion (m=3, n=2, u=2 but different shape)
- r / v: The r can sometimes resemble a v-form
