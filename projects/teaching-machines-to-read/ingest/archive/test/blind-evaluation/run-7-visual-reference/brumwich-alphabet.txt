Hand-Specific Alphabet: Brumwich MS160, Page 10
================================================

This is a two-page opening with small, somewhat cramped handwriting.
Multiple recipes are present on both pages. The hand appears to be a
mixed italic-secretary hand, fairly consistent but small.
The bottom of the left page shows water/stain damage.
Page numbers: 6 (verso left) and 7 (recto right).

MINUSCULES (lowercase)
----------------------
a - Open form; seen in "and", "water", "Take"
b - Ascender with bowl; seen in "bee", "boyle", "blood"
c - Round form; seen in "cold", "cure"
d - Secretary d with left-looping ascender; seen in "and", "drinke"
e - Mixed forms (italic and secretary); seen in "the", "water"
f - With crossbar; seen in "for", "fresh"
g - Open descending tail; seen in "good", "great"
h - Sometimes loops below; seen in "the", "that"
i - Simple minim; seen in "in", "it"
k - Looped; seen in "Take"
l - Simple ascender; seen in "let", "little"
m - Three minims; seen in "made", "much"
n - Two minims; seen in "not", "and"
o - Round; seen in "of", "one"
p - Standard; seen in "put", "pint"
q - With descender; seen in "quarter"
r - Multiple forms; seen in "or", "water"
s - Long-s medially, round s finally; seen in "sugar", "is"
t - Short crossbar; seen in "the", "to"
u/v - Standard distribution; seen in "vpon", "vnto", "use"
w - Double-v; seen in "water", "with"
y - Descender; seen in "you", "your"

MAJUSCULES (uppercase)
----------------------
A - Seen in recipe titles
T - Prominent in "Take"
R - Seen in possible recipe titles

ABBREVIATIONS AND SPECIAL MARKS
---------------------------------
- Superscript letters for abbreviations
- "&" used frequently
- Long-s used in medial position
- Possible thorn usage

CONFUSABLE LETTERS IN THIS HAND
---------------------------------
- The small script size makes many letters difficult to distinguish
- e / o / c: All round letters are potentially confusable
- m / n / u: Minim confusion significant in this small hand
- Long-s / f: Must check carefully for crossbar
- Water damage on left page bottom obscures several passages
