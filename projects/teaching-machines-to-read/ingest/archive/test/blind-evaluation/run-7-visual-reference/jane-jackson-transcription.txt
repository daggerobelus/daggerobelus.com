Confidence Notes
================
This is the most challenging manuscript in the set. Both pages are densely
written with small script, and the left page has severe water damage at
the bottom.

Major difficulties:
- LEFT PAGE: The bottom third is heavily stained with brown water damage
  rendering multiple lines completely illegible.
- Both pages have very small, closely-spaced handwriting.
- The right page, while not water-damaged, has very small script that
  makes many letterforms difficult to trace.

A very high proportion of readings on this page are uncertain. Many words
marked with [?] are based on partial letterforms and should be treated
with extreme caution. This transcription would benefit significantly
from access to a higher-resolution image.

The number of [...] gaps and [word?] flags accurately reflects the
difficulty of reading this manuscript at the available resolution.
Many of the words I have attempted to read could easily be wrong.
