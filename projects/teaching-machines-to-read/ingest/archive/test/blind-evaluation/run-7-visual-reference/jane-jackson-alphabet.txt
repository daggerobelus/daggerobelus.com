Hand-Specific Alphabet: Jane Jackson MS373, Page 20
================================================

This is a two-page opening with a flowing italic-secretary hand.
The left page (verso) has significant water/brown staining at the bottom
that obscures several lines. The right page (recto, numbered 4) is
clearer. Multiple recipes appear on both pages.

MINUSCULES (lowercase)
----------------------
a - Open form; seen in "and", "Take", "water"
b - Standard ascender; seen in "boyle", "butter", "blood"
c - Round; seen in "cold", "cure"
d - Secretary d with leftward loop; seen in "and", "dayes"
e - Mixed italic/secretary; seen in "the", "them"
f - With crossbar; seen in "for", "first"
g - Descending open tail; seen in "good", "great"
h - Loops below line; seen in "the", "that", "this"
i - Simple minim; seen in "in", "it", "is"
k - Looped; seen in "Take"
l - Simple ascender; seen in "let", "like"
m - Three minims; seen in "make", "much"
n - Two minims; seen in "not", "and"
o - Round; seen in "of", "one", "or"
p - Standard; seen in "put"
q - With descender; possibly in "quarter"
r - Multiple forms; seen in "for", "water"
s - Long-s medially, round s finally; seen in "some", "is"
t - Short crossbar; seen in "the", "that", "to"
u/v - Standard distribution; seen in "vpon", "vse"
w - Double-v; seen in "water", "with"
y - With descender; seen in "you", "your"

MAJUSCULES (uppercase)
----------------------
A - Seen in recipe titles and "A"
T - Prominent in "Take"
F - Written as ff or decorative F; seen in "ffor"
R - Seen in some recipe elements

ABBREVIATIONS AND SPECIAL MARKS
---------------------------------
- Superscript letters for abbreviations
- "&" used frequently
- Long-s in medial positions
- Some thorn usage possible (y = th)

CONFUSABLE LETTERS IN THIS HAND
---------------------------------
- e / o: Both fairly round in this hand
- c / r / t: Can be similar
- long-s / f: Check crossbar
- m / n / u: Minim confusion, as always
- Water damage on left page bottom makes many letters impossible to read
- Some ink fading on both pages
