CONFIDENCE NOTES
================================================================

General: This manuscript is very challenging. The compact hand combined
with significant water damage at the bottom of both pages means that
large portions are illegible. I have marked most text as [...] because
the image resolution combined with the small hand size does not allow
confident identification of individual letterforms for most words.

The few words I can read with moderate to high confidence include:

LEFT PAGE:
- "drinke" (line 1): Moderate confidence. Confirmed in vocab reference.
- "ffor the toothache": Moderate confidence. The recipe heading is in
  slightly larger script. "ffor" (double-f capital) and "toothache" are
  both confirmed in vocab reference.
- "ffor the dropsie": Moderate confidence. "dropsie" confirmed in vocab
  reference.
- "gallon" (recipe for dropsie): Moderate confidence. Confirmed in vocab
  reference.
- "dayes": Moderate confidence. Confirmed in vocab reference.
- "suett": Low-moderate confidence. Confirmed in vocab reference.
- "stomach": Low-moderate confidence. Confirmed in vocab reference.

RIGHT PAGE:
- "oyntment": Low-moderate confidence. Confirmed in vocab reference.
- "eyes": Low-moderate confidence. The heading appears to reference eyes.
- "ffor the ache": Moderate confidence. "ache" confirmed in vocab reference.
- "oyle": Low-moderate confidence. Confirmed in vocab reference.
- "boyle": Low-moderate confidence. Attested in vocab reference.

NOTE: The very high proportion of [...] markers in this transcription
reflects the genuine difficulty of reading this manuscript at this image
resolution. The compact hand, close line spacing, and water damage make
it extremely challenging. A higher-resolution image and/or ultraviolet
photography for the water-damaged portions would be necessary for a more
complete transcription.
