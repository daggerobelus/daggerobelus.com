A Tried Cordiall for a Woman in Chilbedd
XXVIII

Take a quarter of a pint of Cowslip water & [...] of
Syrrop of Clove gilliflowers & a pennyworth of Cinnamon
water & a Spoonefull of Claret wine & Nutmegs
[...] mingle all theis togeather and it is a soveran
good cordiall for any especially for one that lyes
in Chilbedd.
