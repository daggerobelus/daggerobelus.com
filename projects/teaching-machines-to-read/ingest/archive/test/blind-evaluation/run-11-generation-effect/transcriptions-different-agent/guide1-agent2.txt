the Leaden plaister
XXVII

Take one pt of oyle olive of the best Take of whit
lead very well [cerused?] withe dust 12 ounces of
Spanish sope incorporate all these well togeather in
an earthen pott well [glased?] & when the oyntmente is
poured that the softe comes abouermost & putt it upon

fastned to a sticke of wood, yt make it fier some

colour but yt must not leave stirringe till the
matter be turned into the coulour of oyle some what
darker, then drop it on a wodden trencher yt it

reines of the back it stops the bludye fluxe
