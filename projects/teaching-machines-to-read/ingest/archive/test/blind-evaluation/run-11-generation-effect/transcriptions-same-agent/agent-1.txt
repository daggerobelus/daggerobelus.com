A Salve for sundry sores called
the Leaden plaister
XXVII

Take one pt of oyle olive of the best Take a pounde of
lead very well centured with dust 12 ounces of
Spanish soper incorporate all those well together in
an earthen pot well glased & when the colour is
parted that the softe comes uppermost & put it upon
a small face of coales continewinge the said fire an
houre & a halfe still stirring it in a bolt of [hors?]
fastned to a stick of wood, yt make yt fire some
what [ugger?] till the reddnes be turned into a gray
colour but yt must not leave stirrings till the
matter be turned into the coulour of oyle somewhat
darker, then drop it on a wooden trencher & if it
neither cleave to yt fingers nor the trencher fassionably
enowgh then [trpp?] bleaden in that, the charcoter of
thereon dipped will last 20 yeares, pt of clary yt [bites?]
this plaister laid to the stomack provokes appetite and

navel, tis a remedy for the [Cholick?] laid to the
raines of the back it stops the bloudy fluxe
