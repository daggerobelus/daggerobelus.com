A Salve for sundry sores called
the Leaden plaister
XXVII

Take one pt of oyle olive of the best Take of culm
lead very well contused with dust 12 ounces of
Spanish sope incorporate all those well together in
an earthen pot well glazed & when the oyntment is
pured that the softe comes uppermost & put it upon
a small face of coals continewinge the said fire an
houre & a halfe still stirringe it on a bolt of brown
fastned to a stick of wood, yt make of fire some
what eager, till the reddnes be turned into a grey
colour but yt must not leave stirringe till the
matter be turned into the coulour of oyle somewhat
darker, then drop it on a wooden trencher & if it
nether cleave to yt fingers nor the trencher [fastonly?]
enoughe then tipps bleedes in that, the [charcoal?] of
thereon [dipned?] will last 20 yeares, pu of glory yt [haxes?]

[sides?], tis a remedy for the [stiche?] laid to the
[reines?] of the back it stops the bloody fluxe
