To Make a powder to heale
the Fistula
Yow muste take a Redd doges heade, and burne itt to powder
skinne heare and all, and make itt in fine powder: and use itt on youre tente:
To make youre tente, you muste take verie fine lint and wett itt
as yow role itt and drie ytt by the fier and by that meanes yow shall make
itt as longe as you will, verie stiffe: as longe as the fistula dothe water
ytt is nott killed: Butt when matter dothe come itt is keled: use to kill it a little
fine coprose on the toppe of youre tente, This is the same
powder that Halle dothe all cures withe.
A greene salve to heale any wounde or cutte
Take valerion sage and floppe: of eche like muche: and pounde
them togeather: and Boyle them in Butter: then straine them, and after
putt to itt some goode new waxe and boyle itt againe: and then itt is
for youre use. probatum
For annye swellinge
Take malmesye redde rose cakes, sallett oyle, and cromes of levened
breade, and boyle them togeather and make a plaister and
Laye itt unto the swellinge and itt will heale itt
probatum
