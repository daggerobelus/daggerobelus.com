A Gargisine for the Quinsie in
the throte
Take a goode handfull of barlye beinge husked and boyle itt in faire
runninge water: withe a chick untill the barlye breake, then putt
thereto a roote or two of Langdebeefe two dates one handefull of ne
reassons of the Sunn: a quarter of a pounde of figges two [ounces]
of coorrance three spoonefulls of wheaton branne, and when these
ar boyled softe straine itt and putt to itt an ounce of sugarr candye
and holde of this a spoonefull or two att a tyme: as warme as
shee can holde: in her mouthe five or sixe tymes a daye:
Applye this undernethe the chinne
Take, of violetts leaves of mallowes and cammamell of eache a good
handfull boyle these untill they bee tender and putt thereto a lillie roote
beynge rosted: stampe all these togeather and make itt in the forme
of a pulltis: puttinge in att the laste three ounces of barrowes grease
and as muche freshe butter withe two ounces of oyle of roses
and use this untill ytt bee broken
