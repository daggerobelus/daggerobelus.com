NOTES

  on what can be learned from examples alone without additional tools.
- The generation effect shows up primarily in CONSISTENCY, not accuracy:
  same-agent spread (excluding outlier) = 0.97 vs different-agent spread = 36.70.
- Several different-agent transcriptions produced outliers (40-65% CER),
  while same-agent results clustered tightly (4 of 5 within 1 percentage point).
