[Left page]

A Salue for sundry sores called
   the Leaden plaister
              XXVII

Take one pt of oyle oliue of the best 1 p*oun*d of
lead very well calcinated w*i*th dust 12 ounces of
Spanish sope incorporate all these well together in
an earthen pot well glased & when the [oyntm*en*t?] is in-
corporated that the sope seemes [aportment?] & put it vpon
a small fire of coales continuewing y*ou*r said fire an
houre & a halfe still stirring it on a bole of boxe
fastned to a stich of wood, y*ou* make y*ou*r fire som*e*
what eager, till the reddnes be turned into a grey
colour but y*ou* must not leaue stirringe till the
matter be turned into the coulour of oyle somw*ha*t
darker, then drop it on a wooden trencher & if it
neith*e*r cleue to y*ou*r finger nor the trencher fassionably
enough, then [dypp*es*?] thicker in that, the thickn*es* or
thinnesse depend will last 20 yeares, p*u*t y*ou*r [oyle?] or [boxe?]
this plaister laid to the stomack [provok*es*?] appetit*e* and
takes away the paines of the Stomack laid to the
[side?], tis a remedy for the [Collick?] laid to the
reines of the back it stops the bloudy flux

[Right page]

openings of the reines [heat?] of the Kidneyes worke*th*

[ffrankes?] ffistions, Impostumes, pusles & [sales?] them in

A Third Cordiall for M*ist*r*es* Owell in Childbedd
              XXVIII

Take a quarter of a pint of Cowslip water & as m*u*ch of
Syrrop of Clouergilliflowers & a pennyeworth of Cinnamon
water an*d* a Spoonefull of Claret wine & [Nutmegs?] & Mormes
Drami mingle all these together and it is a soueran
good cordiall for any, especially for one that lyes
in Childbedd

              Pills for the Gall
              XXIX

[Two?] [words?] good [verified?] and devisde
          After M*ist*r*es* Totter

Take one Handfull of Sallandine & as much bay
salt as can take vp betweene y*ou*r finger & Thumbe &
the iuice of halfe a Lemon stamp y*ou*r together in a
Mortar & straine the iuice out & [smalt?] this place & then
& put some of the hearbs vpon it, vse this 2 or 4
[dayes?] together desistinge it 3 or 4 times in a Day
togeather &c.
