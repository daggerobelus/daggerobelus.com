Confidence Notes:

GENERAL NOTE: This manuscript is written in a very cramped, difficult secretary hand. Both pages are extremely challenging to read. The left page (p. 6) is particularly difficult, with many words where individual letterforms cannot be reliably distinguished. I have used [...] extensively where I cannot trace the pen strokes with any confidence. Many of the readings I have attempted are flagged because the letterforms are genuinely ambiguous.

This transcription has a very high proportion of [...] gaps. This accurately reflects the difficulty of reading this particular hand at this image resolution. A higher-resolution image and extended study would likely resolve many of these readings.

1. The left page contains what appear to be several recipes or medical preparations, but the hand is so compressed that most individual words cannot be read with confidence.

2. The right page is somewhat more legible in places but still very challenging.

3. "M*ist*r*es* [Routley?]" — Right page: The abbreviated title is clear but the surname is uncertain.

4. Many words on both pages are formed with very small, compressed letterforms where individual strokes cannot be distinguished from one another or from strokes belonging to letters on adjacent lines.
