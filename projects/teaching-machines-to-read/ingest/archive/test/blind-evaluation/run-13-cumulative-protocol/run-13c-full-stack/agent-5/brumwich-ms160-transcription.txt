A water of vertues miraculous to hele all maner
of Names lyte or of eyes paine when they
are dusked by eny Rednes
or other wise

Take Drinty of Rosishe in powder very smale
& of the same in powder as muche take a quantitie & runninge
water therin mingle them togeather and sett the water to the
fier whiche muste be faire clay & make a past of ytt &
stoppe your thinge well made aboute of the mouthe
in so litle water it waxeth thicke: seethe all thinge
of the fyer to gett yerein ye the composicione
to the water lett itt stande a litle while
straininge the one thinge of the other
to be in sure as itt ons before

An Oyle to heale a wounde

Take at saint Ihones tyde of a garden grosse, yolde flowres
of Rosemarie whilst crushing greese & halfe oliue oyle
brimstone a handefull and putt all theis in a pott together &
take them in a stronge hette, a space of an yere or att
the leaste an halfe yere then yow maye take of itt
yow neede and make a plaister on a peece of lether
or of a clamorne cloth this is a sure and a meruylous helpe
for euerie sore be itt neuer so oulde putt ytt into
a viall franke a cloath of silke or like moreninge
withe all edge
