NOTES:
- This manuscript is significantly harder to read than the Henslow training
  material. The hand is smaller, more cursive, and less formal.
- Many words remain uncertain. I have marked the most uncertain with [?].
- I have attempted to preserve the scribe's spelling without normalization.
- Where I could not confidently read a word, I transcribed my best
  letter-by-letter reading rather than substituting a familiar word.
- The right page appears to contain medical/household recipes including
  remedies involving mallowes, ffiggs, Langdebeefe, Licoras, and other
  period ingredients.
- The doubled consonant pattern (putt, ytt, itt) appears in places.
- The ff = capital F convention is used (ffor, ffiggs, ffrench).
