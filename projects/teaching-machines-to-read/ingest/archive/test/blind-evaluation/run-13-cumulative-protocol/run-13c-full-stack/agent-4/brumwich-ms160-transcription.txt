then straine itt and keepe itt, and yow must annoynte

A oyle to heale any wounde in 24 houres
Take of the herbage of Sainte Johns worte, goode oyle
olye olyve, white virgins waxe, & of redde saunders
turpentine, a handfull, and halfe a pinte of oyle olyve and
beate them in a morter halfe an houre or more then
putt them in a glasse & sett itt in the sunne
for 7 or 8 dayes in the sunne, and streyninge all
of the herbes and putt the oyle in a glasse and
soe keepe itt, this will heale a cutt or a wounde and
ytt is a common salve This healeth all
manner of woundes and sores in a mervelus sorte [?]
take a cloathe linnen and dipp itt in this oyle
and binde itt vpon the sore: or cutt and soe
yow shall finde itt a goode salve: putt in a notte
of white frankensence [?] & a little of oyle in the moreninge
dresse the wounde or sore and binde itt vp againe and
keepe itt close

ffor an olde bruise or rupture
Take of the oyle of cammamill whiche
is to bee had redye an ounce then Take also
ffor hott eye spauled [?] the yolke of ye one egge lett
