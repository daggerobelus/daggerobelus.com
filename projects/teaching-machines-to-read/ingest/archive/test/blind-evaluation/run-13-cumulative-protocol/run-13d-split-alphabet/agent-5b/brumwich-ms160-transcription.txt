A water of violett leeues excelent for heate
or Inward light [?] or of eyes payne when they
are Inclyned by any reddnes
or Itching soore

Take a Dramme of violetts as fayer and smalle
as you cann and seethe them in faire [?] runninge
water and strayne ytt and putt thereto foure ounces of
good rose water and as muche womans milke
& three pennyworth of white copperis and as muche
of Camphere as a beane [?] & mingell all theis
in an olde ale Cuppe of steele and sett ytt
vppon hott Imbers and lett ytt seeth a walme
or two & so putt ytt vpp in a close glasse
for to bee occupied to the eyes withe a feather
and so vse it as cause before [?]

A oile to bee vsed to a greene wounde

Take a handfull of herbe of grace, well beaten
& a quarterne of sallad oyle & a sponefull of honye
putt them wherin white vynneger was & steepe ytt together
halfe withe a handefull of an ounce of oyle of hypericone [?]
laye them in together in a glasse & sett ytt in the
sunne thirtie or fortie dayes In a morning take
of ytt and as occasion serueth anoynte the
wounde and a greene salue att a morning luke
warme & in a cloathe putt on and putt it withe
an olde freshe grasse [?] or clothe in like morning
ouerlaid againe

A salve to heale [?]

The makinge of a soveraigne oyntment of greate
verttue of whiche it is for a remedye of newe woundes & cuttes
brought me receaued from venice by
a Surgione whiche hath greate practise of the curring
of woundes and of other hurtes also in like
manner of all other kindes of sores & sugare [?]

To laye a birdes bone to purpose [?]
Take of the olde ale a reasonable quantitye
& boile ytt on a pann & then strayne then take & putt
it hott & ye [?] shoulde do her good it is ytt lett
& was drie had some to the youre [?]
good oyntment Take a plaister smaller & like
to the oyle sole [?] & of oure rose oyntment thereto
The whiche plaister or oyntment his makinge
contribution & greate verttue heare nexte & folowinge
& to the laste recipe for ye [?] page To & of this
daye probatum
