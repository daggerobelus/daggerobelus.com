CONFIDENCE NOTES
================

This manuscript presents two major challenges: (1) the hand, while clearer than Brumwich, is still a rapid secretary hand with small letterforms, and (2) significant water damage renders the bottom third of both pages almost entirely illegible.

MAJOR ISSUES:

1. WATER DAMAGE: The bottom approximately one-third of both pages has heavy brown water staining. In these areas, the ink has been partially dissolved and the text is unreadable. I have marked these as passage-level illegibility rather than attempting word-by-word reconstruction.

2. LEFT PAGE: The upper portion has several short recipes that are partially legible. I can identify recipe structures and some opening words but many individual words within recipes cannot be resolved.

3. RIGHT PAGE: Somewhat more legible in the upper portion. Recipe headings and "Take" openings are visible but much of the body text is difficult.

4. Overall, I estimate that fewer than 25% of the words on these pages can be read with confidence. The water damage alone accounts for roughly a third of the text being completely unreadable, and much of the remaining text is in a difficult hand.

5. The recipe titles/headings are generally slightly more legible than the body text, likely because they were written with more care.

NOTE: This transcription has the lowest confidence of all five manuscripts. The combination of water damage and a rapid hand means that most of what I have provided as [word?] readings should be treated as very uncertain. An in-person examination of the physical manuscript would be essential for any serious transcription work.
