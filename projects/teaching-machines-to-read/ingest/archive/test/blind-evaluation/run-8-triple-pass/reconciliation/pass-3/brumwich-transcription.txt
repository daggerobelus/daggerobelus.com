CONFIDENCE NOTES
================

This is by far the most difficult manuscript of the five. The hand is extremely rapid and compressed, the ink varies in darkness, and the vocabulary is specialized.

MAJOR ISSUES:

1. LEFT PAGE: The upper portion has several recipes that are partially legible. I can identify general recipe structures (Take..., ingredients, instructions) but many individual words cannot be resolved to specific letterforms.

2. LEFT PAGE, lower half: The text becomes even more compressed and difficult. Multiple passages are marked [...] because I cannot distinguish individual letterforms.

3. RIGHT PAGE: Somewhat more legible in places but still very challenging. Recipe headings are slightly clearer than body text.

4. Overall, I estimate that fewer than 40% of the words on this page can be read with high confidence. The remaining 60%+ are either flagged uncertain or marked illegible.

5. The page numbering (6 and 7) is visible and clear.

6. Recipe structures are visible even where individual words are not — I can see "Take" openings, ingredient lists, and procedural instructions.

NOTE: Many of the [word?] flagged readings in this transcription are very uncertain. Where I have provided a reading, it represents my best interpretation of the visible pen strokes, but I have low confidence in the majority of these readings. A more experienced paleographer examining this manuscript in person would likely produce a substantially different (and more complete) transcription.
