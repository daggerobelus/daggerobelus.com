OVERALL NOTE: Brumwich MS 160 shows by far the lowest inter-pass agreement
of any manuscript in this test. The extremely small hand, dense layout, and
water damage result in the majority of words having either 3-way disagreement
or being marked illegible by multiple passes. Reliable consensus is possible
only for the most common function words, recipe structural words ("Take,"
"of," "and," "&"), and a small number of ingredient names that all three
passes recognized (Cowslipps, violetts, Treacle, sugar).
