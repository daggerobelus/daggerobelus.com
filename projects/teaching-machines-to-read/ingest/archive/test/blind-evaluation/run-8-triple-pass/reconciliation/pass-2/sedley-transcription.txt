A Salue for sundry sores called

XXVII

Take one pt of oyle oliue of the best [&?] of [whit?]

Spanish sope incorporate all these well together in
an earthen pot not glased & when the [colour?] is
[parted?] that the sope seemes [eporenett?] & put it upon
a small face of coales continewinge the said fire an

matter be turned into the coulour of oyle some what
darker, then drop it on a wooden trencher & if it

enoughe then [dippt?] therein in that the [character?] or
thereon [dipped?] will last 20 yeares, pt of clary yt [hase?]

takes away the paines of the stomack laid to the
[side?] tis a remedy for the [collicke?] laid to the
raines of the back it stops the bloodye fluxe
