CONFIDENCE NOTES
================

GENERAL NOTE: This manuscript presents two major challenges: (1) the
italic-secretary hybrid hand, while clearer than some manuscripts,
still contains many ambiguous letterforms at the word level, and (2)
significant water/stain damage renders the lower portions of both
pages largely or entirely illegible.

HIGH CONFIDENCE READINGS:
- Recipe headings ("ffor paine in the ioyntes", "ffor to breake an Impostume")
- Common words: "and", "the", "of", "in", "with", "Take"
- "horehound" (a known medicinal herb)
- "butter", "vineger"
- "togeathor" (consistent spelling)
- "Apotecaries"

MEDIUM CONFIDENCE READINGS:
1. "[liuerwort?]" - Medium confidence. Known herb, letterforms partially clear.
2. "[Tamarisk?]" - Medium confidence. Known plant, some letterforms visible.
3. "[Comfrey?]" - Medium confidence. Known herb used in recipes.
4. "[Impostume?]" - Medium-high confidence. Standard medical term.
5. "[Splene?]" - Medium confidence. Anatomical term (spleen).
6. "[Emplaster?]" - Medium confidence. Known medical term (plaster/poultice).

LOW CONFIDENCE / ILLEGIBLE:
7. The left page lower section (roughly lines 15-25) becomes increasingly
   difficult, and the final 4-5 lines are rendered illegible by water damage.
8. The right page contains many uncertain readings throughout, with the
   lower section almost entirely lost to water damage.
9. Many recipe attributions and specific ingredient measurements are
   uncertain or illegible.
10. "[meddinge?]" - Low confidence. The recipe title at top of right page
    is difficult to read.
11. Numerous words in the middle sections of both pages are tentative
    readings at best.

WATER DAMAGE: The most significant factor affecting this transcription.
Approximately 30-40% of the visible text on these two pages is partially
or fully obscured by water staining / foxing damage.
