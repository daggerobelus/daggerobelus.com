CONFIDENCE NOTES
================

GENERAL NOTE: This is by far the most difficult manuscript in the set.
The writing is extremely small and dense, with many words that I cannot
read with any confidence. A very large number of readings are uncertain
or illegible.

The transcription above contains an unusually high number of bracketed
uncertain readings. Many of these should be considered tentative at best.
In several places I have provided my best reading based on visible
letterforms, but the confidence is generally low.

SPECIFIC NOTES:

LEFT PAGE:
1. The first several lines at top are partially cut off or very faded.
2. Multiple recipe sections are present but boundaries are hard to determine.
3. "Treakle" - Medium confidence; a known medicinal ingredient (theriac).
4. The section about a gargle/gout remedy is partially readable.
5. Lines 15-22 are among the most difficult, with very compressed writing.

RIGHT PAGE:
6. The top of the right page has some text that is partially obscured.
7. "A receipte for one commodament to Master Beniamen Lougthor" -
   Low-medium confidence. This appears to be a recipe attribution but
   the names are very uncertain.
8. "Ivye" - Medium confidence; ivy is a known ingredient.
9. "Apotacaryes" - Medium confidence; recognizable word but letterforms small.
10. The recipe for an "ague" at bottom right is partially readable.
11. "violetts" - Medium confidence; a common recipe ingredient.

OVERALL: Given the extreme difficulty of this manuscript, many readings
should be independently verified. I estimate less than 50% of the text
on these pages has been read with high confidence.
