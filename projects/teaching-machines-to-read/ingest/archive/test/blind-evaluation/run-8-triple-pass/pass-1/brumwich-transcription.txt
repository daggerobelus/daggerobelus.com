Confidence Notes
================

This is by far the most challenging manuscript of the five. The extremely
small hand, dense text, cramped layout, and water damage on the left page
make reliable transcription very difficult.

OVERALL ASSESSMENT:
- Left page: Approximately 40-50% of words could be read with any confidence.
  The bottom portion is largely illegible due to water staining.
- Right page: Approximately 30-40% of words could be read with confidence.
  The text is slightly larger but still very dense.

KEY ISSUES:
1. The hand is extremely small, making minim-based letters (m, n, u, i)
   nearly impossible to distinguish reliably.
2. The dense layout means letterforms from adjacent lines interfere with
   each other.
3. The left page has significant water damage at the bottom.
4. Many of the flagged words represent my best paleographic reading, but
   with low confidence due to the size of the letterforms.
5. I have marked entire passages as illegible where I could not reliably
   read more than half the words, rather than attempting word-by-word
   reconstruction from fragments and context.
6. The right page appears to contain several recipe titles and recipes,
   but the small hand makes headers hard to distinguish from body text.
