CONFIDENCE NOTES
================================================================

This manuscript presented extreme difficulty due to two factors:
1. The small, dense handwriting requires careful letterform analysis
2. Significant water/stain damage renders the lower third of both pages
   almost entirely illegible

The water damage is particularly severe on the left page, where brown
staining obscures most text below approximately line 15. The right page
has somewhat less damage but still loses significant text in the lower
portion.

In undamaged areas, the hand is reasonably clear but still challenging
due to the image resolution.

Flagged words: Due to the overwhelming number of uncertain or illegible
readings, individual justification for each flag is not practical. The
primary reasons for flagging are:
- Water damage obscuring letterforms
- Small script size making minim-based distinctions difficult
- Compressed text at line endings
- Interference from staining making ink strokes ambiguous
