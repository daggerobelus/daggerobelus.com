================================================================
CONFUSION RISK RANKING (highest to lowest)
================================================================

1. e / o — HIGHEST RISK
   Why: In this small hand, both letters are tiny rounded forms that are
   nearly indistinguishable without magnification. This is the most
   significant challenge for transcribing this manuscript.

2. long-s (ſ) / f
   Why: Both tall with ascender/descender. The crossbar distinction is
   subtle in this small hand.

3. n / u
   Why: Both two-minim forms. In the small script, the directional
   distinction (arch vs. curve) is particularly hard to see.

4. minim confusion (m/in/ni/iu/ui)
   Why: The small size makes minim-based letters especially challenging.
   Three minims could be m, in, ni, iu, etc.

5. c / t / e
   Why: All small forms that can blur together in this hand.

6. h / b / l
   Why: All have ascenders; in small script the distinguishing features
   (bowl, curve, plain stroke) are harder to detect.

7. r / v
   Why: Standard r can resemble v in small script.

8. d / cl
   Why: Secretary d's looped ascender, though in small script the loop
   may be compressed and harder to distinguish from c+l.
