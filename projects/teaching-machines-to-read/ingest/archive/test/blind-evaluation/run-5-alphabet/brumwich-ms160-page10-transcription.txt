CONFIDENCE NOTES
================================================================

This manuscript presented extreme difficulty due to the small, dense
handwriting at the available image resolution. Many words could not
be confidently read because individual letterforms were not
distinguishable at this scale. I have been conservative in my
readings, preferring to mark text as illegible rather than guess.

Flagged words: The sheer volume of uncertain readings makes individual
justification impractical, but the general reasons are:
- Small script size makes minim-based letters (i, m, n, u) nearly
  impossible to distinguish reliably
- The e/o confusion is pervasive at this scale
- Long-s/f distinction is very difficult in small script
- Many words are flagged because only some letterforms are visible
  while others are too small to read with confidence

Words marked fully illegible [...]: approximately 245 positions
Flagged words [word?]: approximately 128
Flag percentage (flagged words / total attempted words): 70.3%

NOTE: The extremely high flag percentage reflects the genuine difficulty
of reading this small hand at this image resolution. A higher-resolution
image would likely resolve many of these readings. The transcription
above represents my best effort at reading individual letterforms,
with honest flagging of uncertainty.
