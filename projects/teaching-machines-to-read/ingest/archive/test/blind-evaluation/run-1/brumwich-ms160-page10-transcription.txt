========================================
CONFIDENCE NOTES
========================================

General observations:
- This manuscript is written in a somewhat cramped hand that is
  difficult to read at this resolution
- The left page (6) has text that grows increasingly difficult to
  read toward the bottom
- The right page (7) is somewhat clearer but still challenging
- The hand appears to be a mixed secretary/italic hand

Left page:
- The opening recipe about "Endiue" (endive) and "ciccoris" (chicory)
  is the clearest text on this page
- "Dynary of Treach" may be "Denary of Treacle" — a common medicinal
  compound — but I have transcribed what I see
- The recipe about brimstone and antimony appears to be an alchemical
  or medical preparation
- The bottom portion includes what appears to be a veterinary recipe
  ("Tell any a blind horse")

Right page:
- Contains recipes for aches and joint pain ("Gonnes in the Ioyntes")
- Attribution "Mr Bostley" is uncertain — this could be a different name
- The syrup recipe and the recipe for aches are the most legible
  portions

Many readings are flagged due to the cramped hand, faded ink, and
difficulty distinguishing individual letterforms at this resolution.
The manuscript would benefit from higher-resolution imaging for more
accurate transcription.
