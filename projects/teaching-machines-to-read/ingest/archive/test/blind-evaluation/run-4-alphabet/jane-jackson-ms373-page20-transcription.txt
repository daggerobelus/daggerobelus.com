CONFIDENCE NOTES
============================================================

CRITICAL NOTE: This manuscript page is the most challenging in the set. Both pages suffer from extensive water/staining damage, particularly in the lower portions. The left page has severe water damage beginning approximately one-third from the bottom, and the right page has significant staining throughout.

I have marked the vast majority of readings as uncertain because:
1. The water staining has caused ink to bleed, fade, or become obscured across large portions of the text.
2. Where individual words are readable, surrounding context is often missing, making it impossible to verify readings through cross-reference.
3. Per the transcription instructions, I have marked illegible passages with [...] rather than attempting to reconstruct text from fragments.

Specific notes:
- The left page appears to contain recipes for treating ailments (burns/scalding, corns, and other conditions).
- "ffor Cornes" on the left page is one of the more clearly readable headings (ff = capital F, "Cornes" = corns).
- The right page (numbered 9) contains additional recipes, but the damage makes sustained reading very difficult.
- Several recipe headings are partially visible but the body text beneath them is often heavily damaged.
- The hand itself, where visible, is fairly legible — an italic-influenced secretary hand. The difficulty is almost entirely due to physical damage to the manuscript rather than difficulty of the handwriting.
- Approximately 40-50% of the text on these two pages is illegible due to water damage. Rather than fabricating plausible readings, I have followed the instruction to mark illegible text with [...].
- "Elayne" in the left page is uncertain — could be a proper name or a variant spelling of a medical/plant term.
