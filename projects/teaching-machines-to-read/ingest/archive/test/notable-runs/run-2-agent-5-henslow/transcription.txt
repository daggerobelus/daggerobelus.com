My lorde of Caunterberries
Broathe for a Consumption
Take like as before sauinge putt thereto a hansfull of violet leaues
and a hansfull of betteony leaues Brouage leaues and strawberrie leaues, with
the gather of euerie one alike quantitie.

For the pyles
Take one handfull of mallow leaues, and wrappe them in a Coleworte
leafe, and rake them in the imbers vntill they bee well rosted
then take them, and putt awaye the Cosworte leafe, and grinde
them in a mortar, and putt to yt oyle of roses, and grinde them togeather
then take it and laye it to the pyles with a trosser, and vse this
morninge and euyninge.

For the flixe
Take horsedunge new made and frie it withe sweete butter
and putt it in a Cloathe and laye it to the fundament as hott
as you maye suffer yt, and this oppen vntill you haue ease.

For Deffues
Take blacke wooll whiche growes vppon the flanke
of a sheepe, and tarre, and mingell them togeather
and putt them into the eare.
