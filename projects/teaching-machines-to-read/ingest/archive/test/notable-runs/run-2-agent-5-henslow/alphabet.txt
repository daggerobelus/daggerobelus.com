HAND-SPECIFIC ALPHABET — single scribe, mixed secretary/italic recipe hand
==========================================================================
Headings are in a large display script; body in a smaller current hand.

a  — round, single-compartment; cup open at top. Ex: Take, hansfull, gathered, flanke.
     Confusion: with o when ink heavy; with u when narrow.
b  — looped ascender. Ex: betteony, before, butter, blacke. Confusion: with h (h has
     descender below line, b does not).
c  — small open hook, sits low. Ex: Cosworte, Coleworte, decoction. Confusion: with t, r.
d  — looped ascender curving back left (secretary). Ex: drinke, lorde, sodden.
e  — secretary reversed-e (open loop) AND italic e both appear. Ex: leaues, before, ease.
     Confusion: with o.
f  — long-s with crossbar; ff at word start = capital F (For, flixe, fundament, frie).
g  — looped descender. Ex: gathered, growes, grinde, dunge.
h  — tall loop with descender dipping below line. Ex: thereto, hansfull, haste, hott.
i  — minim, dotted inconsistently. j not distinctly used.
k  — looped, elaborate. Ex: Take, blacke, drinke, flanke.
l  — single tall loop. Ex: lorde, leaues, mallow, oyle, violet. Doubled: hansfull, woolle.
m  — three minims. Ex: morninge, mortar, mingell.
n  — two minims. Ex: betteony, consumption, fundament.
o  — round closed. Ex: lorde, oyle, oppen, roses, broathe.
p  — descender; pp in oppen; "special" p not strongly marked here. Ex: putt, pyles, ploughe.
q  — with u: quantitie.
r  — secretary 2-shaped r AND a v-like r. Ex: mortar, roses, drinke, grinde. Confusion: v.
s  — long-s (medial/initial) like f w/o crossbar; round/sigma s at word end. Ex: leaues,
     roses, suffer, sweete. Terminal -es graph appears (leaues, roses, growes).
t  — crossed; sits low. Ex: Take, take, take. Confusion: c.
u/v — v at word start, u medial: violet, vntill, vse, vpon; haue-type medial u (saurine,
     suffer). Scribe uses v initially.
w  — double-v with loops. Ex: wrappe, woolle, growes, sweete.
x  — flixe, defluxe.
y  — pyles, oyle, euerie(y), morbefore.
z  — not observed.

LIGATURES / GRAPHS:
- th: written out (not thorn) — the, them, thereto.
- ff initial = F: For, flixe, frie, fundament.
- terminal -es graph: leaues, roses, growes, berries.
- doubled final consonants common: hansfull, woolle, putt, hott.

ORTHOGRAPHIC HABITS OF THIS SCRIBE:
- v at word beginnings (vntill, vpon, vse, violet), u medially.
- i used (no clear j).
- terminal -e frequent: lorde, sweete, leaue, oyle, broathe, swete~sweete vary.
- doubled final consonants: hansfull/handfull, woolle, putt, hott, mallow, mingell.
- spelling -inge for -ing: morninge, euyninge.
- th written, no thorn.
- &c. / etc. not used; "&" forms appear (and).
