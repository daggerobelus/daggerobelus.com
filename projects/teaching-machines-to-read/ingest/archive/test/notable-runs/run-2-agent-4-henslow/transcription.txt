My lorde of Caunterberries
Brothe for a Consumptione
Take, like as before sauinge putt thereto a handfull of violett leaues
langdebeeffe leaues Brorrage leaues and strawberrie leaues, knitt
togeather of euerie one alike quantitye.

For the pyles
Take one handefull of mollett leaues, and wrappe them in a Colewort
leafe, and rake them in the imbers vntill they bee well rosted
then take them, and putt awaye the Colewotte leafe, and grinde
them in a morter, and putt to ytt oyle of roses, and grinde them togeather
then take itt and laye itt to the pyles withe a trusser, and vse this
morninge and euenynge.

For the flixe
Take horsedounge new made and frie itt withe sweete butter
and putt itt in a clothe and laye itt to the fundamente as hott
as yow maye suffer ytt, and sitte vppon vntill yow haue ease.

For Deffnes
Take blacke woole, whiche growes vppon the flanke
of a sheepe and tarre and mingell them togeather
and putt them into the eare.
