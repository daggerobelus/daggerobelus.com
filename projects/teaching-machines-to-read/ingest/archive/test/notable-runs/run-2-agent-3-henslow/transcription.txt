My lorde of Cawnterberries
Brothe for a Consumptione
Take. like as before saunes putt thereto a hansfull of violet leaues
and Cowslippe leaues Borrage leaues and strawberrie leaues, with
iie gather of euerie one alike quantitie.
For the pyles
Take one handefull of mossell leaues, and wrappe them in a Colewort
leafe, and rake them in the embers vntill they gee well rosted
then take them, and putt awaye the Coleworte leafe, and grinde
them in a mortier, and putt to yt oyle of roses, and grinde them togeather
then take it and laye yt ith the pyles withe a trosser, and vse this
morninge and eueninge.
For the flixe
Take horsedunge new made and frie ith withe sweete butter
and putt ith in a Clothe and laye yt to the fundament as hott
as you maye suffer yt, and frie vppon vntill yow haste ease.
For Defluxes
Take blacke wooll, whiche groweth vppon the flanke
of a sheepe and taree and mingell them togeather
and putt them inte the eare.
