HAND-SPECIFIC ALPHABET — secretary/mixed hand, recipe book
==========================================================
Headings written in a larger display hand; body in a smaller running
secretary hand. Same scribe.

a  — single-compartment, open; cf. "Take", "handefull", "anoynt".
     Confusion: o, u when hurried.
b  — looped ascender; "before", "Brothe", "blacke", "butter".
c  — open c, can resemble r/t; "Consumptione", "Colewort".
d  — looped d, ascender curves left; "lorde", "made", "grinde".
e  — secretary reversed-e and round-e both used; very common terminal -e.
     "leaues", "before", "the".
f  — long descender, crossbar; "flixe", "fundament", "fie".
     ff at word start = capital F: "For", "Fluxe".
g  — looped tail; "grinde", "groweth".
h  — tall with descender loop; "thereto", "the", "horsedunge".
i  — minim, dotted variably; "violet", "iuyce".
j  — not clearly distinct; i/j interchangeable.
k  — looped; "Take", "blacke", "milke".
l  — looped ascender; "lorde", "violet", "wooll".
m  — three minims; "made", "mortar", "morninge".
n  — two minims; "anoynt", "fundament".
o  — round; "lorde", "wooll", "oyle".
p  — descender; "puff", "pyles", "puttinge". special-p possible in pro-/per-.
q  — "quantitie".
r  — secretary r and 2-shaped r; "Brothe", "roses", "mortar".
s  — long-s (f without crossbar) medial/initial; round-s terminal.
     "leaues", "roses", "violet".
t  — crossed t; "Take", "thereto", "violet".
u/v — v at word start, u medial: "violet", "vse", "vppon", "iuyce",
     "leaues" (=leaues), "haue"-type.
w  — "wrappe", "wooll", "with".
x  — "flixe", "fluxe".
y  — "pyles", "oyle", "anoynt", "yt" (=it), "ye".
z  — none observed.

LIGATURES / GRAPHS:
- th common.
- terminal -es graph on "leaues", "pyles", "roses".
- "yt" = it / "ytt"; "wth"/"with".

SCRIBE'S ORTHOGRAPHIC HABITS:
- v at word beginning (vse, vppon), u in middle (iuyce, leaues).
- Frequent doubled consonants: "wooll", "puff", "vppon", "wrappe".
- Terminal -e very common: "lorde", "Brothe", "leafe", "made".
- "yt" for "it"; "ith"/"itt" forms.
- -ie / -y endings: "quantitie", "morninge", "eueninge", "easie".
- Phonetic spellings: "Cawnterberries", "horsedunge", "sweete".
- "wch" not seen; uses "whiche".
