To dry Lemons

Take your lemons and pare them as thin as you
can and lay them in water a day and a night
shifteing as often as e water is Bitter then
scoure them wth salt and water betweene your
hands then boyle them very tender and shifting
your water if it be bitter then take them
cleane out of e water and put them into a

haue Patience or not medle wth it thus you
dry oranges soeall

days after the Bite.
