A Goodd Meddcine againste the
plague approoved
Amongst, other excelent and approoved medicines for the pestilence
there is none more worthy available when the sore dothe appeere then
to take a Cockke pullett or chicken and lett the feathers of the taile of
the highest parte bee plucked off untill the rumpe bee bare, then holde
the saide bare of the pullett unto the sore, and the pullett will labore
for life and gape: and in the ende will dye: then have one other pullett
and doe the like to the patiente: and if that dye, yett still applye
the patiente with pulletts so longe as annye doe dye: for when the
poyesone by the saide chickens is drawne foorthe: then the saide
chickens that bee offered thereunto will live: and then the sore
presentlye will asswage: and the partie foorthewithe recoouerethe
this meddcine is nessessarie to drive the venome from the harte:

For a sore legge

To make sage ale to teake the water out of the Belly of any body boddy
Teake five or six gallones of good Ale but not of the strongest and putt it into a

before your capp that when you drawe your Ale the spice nor anythinge com
into your cupp: and drinke of this morninge and eveninge and at your moath
in the spring of the yeare for the space of one moneth and this drinke will
draw out the water out of any bodyes Belly.

To make a good treate for Sores or wonds woonds
Teake a quantety of deeres Shuett and half so much wax and Rosen as

Alos Ungentom
This Intreat is called the flowre Oyntment for it is good for all malledyes
that are written herafter that is to saye for olde woonds and new foramongst
amongst all entreats it is most Clensing and most and ingendereth
flesh and healeth more in a weeke then other doe in a moneth and it suffereth
no Corruption to be in a woonde nor no evell fleshe to be ingendred therin and
it is good for the head atch and for all mannor of Impostumes in the head
and in the body or for bublinge in the fawes or for the Cheekes or for Sinoes
Brouken or spronge and it draweth out any thorne or broaken boane
or what is on a woonde it is good for bytinge or stickinge of any envious beaste
and for bytinge of a madd dogg and rotteth and healeth all mannor of botches
without forth it is good for a festured Canker or Nolemetangere and it draweth
out all manner of in the liver the Splene or the Raines it breakethe the
Impostume it is good for bublinge in the members and it Ceaseth a Flux men
strions layd one a womans navell drawne one a linnen cloeth and espetiall
good plaster for a pestelent botch and for Hmereds it healeth them shure.
The makinge of this medicin
Take Rosen and perrosen of each halfe a pounde of virgin wax and
Frankinsence of each a quarter of a pounde of Mastick one ounce of harts
tallowe a quarter of a pounde of Camphyer two dramms melt that scerch
your powder well and boyle it one a sad fyre altogeither and straine it
through a Canves bagg made like an Apoticaries bagg into a pottell of
White Wyne then lett it coole till it be blood warme then putt therto a
quarter of a pounde of turpentine littell and littell evermore stirringe
it well untill it be through colde then make it upp in Roles and keepe
it to your use for the best salve of all others
