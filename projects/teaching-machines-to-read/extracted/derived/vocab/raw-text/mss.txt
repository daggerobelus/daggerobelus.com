Take half a pound of refined suger beaten
small and in a siluer dish or earthen stroing
the bottome of the dish with suger sprinkle
faire watter upon it lay half a pound of plums
one by on upon the bottom of the dish and
couer them all ouer with the remainder
of the suger and againe sprinkle more
watter upon them couer them with a dish
and put them in an ouen that is hot
and let them stand till they skins begin
to be a littill broken then tak them foer
and turne them one by one and let them
stand untell the next day then straine
them from the sirup and lay them one
by one upon a seue and set them into an
ouen that is but warm and let them stand
half a day then turne them and let thrm

keep them for your use. the sirup of this

To Make Red Quince Marmalade

Take the Best Quinces pare & Quarter
'em & Cutt Out the Core & Take the
Stoney part and put them In Water.
as you Cutt 'em Weigh them & Take
Their Weight In Sugar. put 2 parts of it
to them & a Quart of water to Every
3 pound of Quinces. Set em Over a Soft
fire Covering them Very Close. Boyl the
pairings & Cores in as much Water as
Will Cover em Till it Iellys. Strain 'em
put it Into e Quinces. & Boil till Allmost
Red Enough & Break the Quince With
the Edge of your spoon. putt in e Rest of
yor Sugar. Boil it Quick. And Scum it
when off the fire.

To take of a funch of dry mould being
in &af Tobacco damadge by salt water.

Put two pound of treacle in four quarts
of water and two quarts of Vinig. Beat
it well togather till the treacle is all
disovld. then dry it very well in the Bave
then stripe it. and sprincle. e tobacco
wth that liquor to make it in a working
case before you cut it and make it very
moist wth the said liquor after it is cutt
then take 8 oz. of Iemeco pepper beat it
to a fine powder and mixt it wth the
qt of jli wheight then dry it over the fire
in e dryer till it be very dry the nature
of the liquor will make it give again.
this will make it smoak well and give
it a pleasant sent.
