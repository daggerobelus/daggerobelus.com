HAND-SPECIFIC ALPHABET: Sedley MS 534, Page 13
=================================================

General Observations:
- Two-page spread visible (left and right pages)
- Small, somewhat compressed secretary hand with italic influence
- Multiple hands may be present — the title/recipe headings appear different from body text
- Numbered recipes (XXVII, XXVIII, XXIX) in Roman numerals
- Some marginal annotations visible
- Ink varies — some sections darker/clearer than others
- Writing is relatively small, requiring careful letter-by-letter reading

LOWERCASE LETTERS
-----------------

a: Open-topped form with a rounded bowl, sometimes angular.
   Examples: "Salue", "Take", "all"
   Confusion risks: Can resemble u in minim sequences
   Variants: Generally consistent

b: Ascender with rounded bowl.
   Examples: "best", "body", "butter"
   Confusion risks: Could be confused with l or h
   Variants: One main form

c: Small open curve, fairly consistent.
   Examples: "cerecloath", "colour", "cordial"
   Confusion risks: Could be confused with e or r
   Variants: One form

d: Secretary d with leftward-looping ascender.
   Examples: "and", "dust", "dipped"
   Confusion risks: Loop could suggest cl
   Variants: One form

e: Secretary e — reversed loop, sometimes very small.
   Examples: "the", "best", "take"
   Confusion risks: Easily confused with o in this small hand
   Variants: Generally consistent

f: Descender with crossbar.
   Examples: "for", "fire", "fine"
   Confusion risks: Long-s (lacks full crossbar)
   Variants: One form

g: Small bowl with descending curved tail.
   Examples: "good", "great", "glass"
   Confusion risks: Could be confused with y
   Variants: One form

h: Ascender with descending curve below baseline.
   Examples: "the", "that", "houres"
   Confusion risks: Could resemble b
   Variants: One form

i: Short minim, sometimes dotted.
   Examples: "in", "it", "incorporate"
   Confusion risks: Minim confusion with n, u, m
   Variants: One form

k: Tall ascender with angular strokes.
   Examples: "Take" (k)
   Confusion risks: Could be confused with h
   Variants: One form

l: Tall straight ascender.
   Examples: "lead", "last", "laid"
   Confusion risks: Could be confused with b, h
   Variants: One form

m: Three connected minims.
   Examples: "make", "must", "mortar"
   Confusion risks: Standard minim confusion
   Variants: One form

n: Two connected minims.
   Examples: "not", "new", "nor"
   Confusion risks: Minim confusion with u
   Variants: One form

o: Round closed bowl.
   Examples: "of", "or", "one"
   Confusion risks: Could be confused with e
   Variants: One form

p: Descender with bowl.
   Examples: "put", "plaster", "pint"
   Confusion risks: Special p abbreviations possible
   Variants: Standard p

q: Bowl with descending rightward tail.
   Examples: "quarter"
   Confusion risks: Could be confused with g
   Variants: One form

r: Multiple forms — standard and 2-shaped.
   Examples: "red", "roses", "remedy"
   Confusion risks: 2-shaped r could be confused with v
   Variants: At least two forms

s: Long-s medially/initially, round-s finally.
   Examples: long-s in "salue"; round-s in "roses"
   Confusion risks: Long-s vs f
   Variants: Two forms

t: Short vertical with crossbar.
   Examples: "the", "take", "that"
   Confusion risks: Could be confused with c
   Variants: One form

u/v: v initial, u medial per convention.
   Examples: "vpon", "vnto", "leaues"
   Confusion risks: u/n minim confusion; v/r confusion
   Variants: Positional variants

w: Wide double-v form.
   Examples: "with", "will", "water"
   Confusion risks: Minimal
   Variants: One form

x: Crossing diagonals.
   Examples: rare on this page
   Confusion risks: Minimal

y: Descender curving below line.
   Examples: "you", "your"
   Confusion risks: Thorn confusion
   Variants: One form

z: Not clearly observed.

COMMON COMBINATIONS AND LIGATURES
----------------------------------

th: Connected t+h, very common. Thorn may appear.
st: s+t connected in words like "plaster"
-es: Terminal -es graph (looped downstroke)
-er: Hook upstroke
-tion: Abbreviated with tilde
&: Ampersand present
wth: "with" abbreviated as w + superscript th

RANKED CONFUSION RISK SUMMARY (Highest to Lowest)
---------------------------------------------------

1. e / o — Very high risk in this small hand
2. Minim sequences (m/n/u/i) — Dense text makes minims hard to distinguish
3. long-s / f — Standard risk
4. c / e / r — Small forms can overlap
5. d / cl — Looped d
6. h / b — Similar ascenders
7. y / thorn
8. r / v — 2-shaped r
