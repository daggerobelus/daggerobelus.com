CONFIDENCE NOTES
================

This manuscript is significantly more difficult to read than Henslow. The hand is rapid and compressed, the vocabulary is learned/medical, and many words are partially unclear.

MAJOR UNCERTAINTIES:

1. Lines 1-7 (opening paragraph on temperature/properties): The general sense is clear — discussing lavender's hot and dry qualities — but several individual words are uncertain due to the rapid hand and specialized medical vocabulary.

2. "[thinne?]" (line 2) — The letterforms suggest "thinne" but could be another word.

3. "[airie?]" (line 2) — Appears to describe spiritual/airy parts; letterforms partially clear.

4. "[spiritiall?]" (line 3) — Long word with ambiguous minims in the middle.

5. "Catalepsie" (line 12) — This reading is fairly confident; the word is clear and refers to a medical condition.

6. "[soune?]" (line 14) — Could be "swound" (swoon) but the letterforms show what appears to be s-o-u-n-e.

7. Lines 16-end: The bottom half of the page becomes increasingly difficult. The scribe appears to be writing more rapidly, and many words involve medical/pharmaceutical terminology that I cannot confirm from letterforms alone.

8. Multiple words in the final 8 lines are very uncertain. I have flagged many with [?] markers. Where I could not make out letterforms at all, I have used [...].

9. The page appears to continue ("that" at bottom right), indicating this is a mid-passage page.

10. "[mashinge?]" — Unclear; could be "measure" or "meaning" — the minims are very difficult.

NOTE: The bottom third of this page has a high density of uncertain readings. Many of the flagged words are my best attempt at the letterforms but I have low confidence in several of them.
