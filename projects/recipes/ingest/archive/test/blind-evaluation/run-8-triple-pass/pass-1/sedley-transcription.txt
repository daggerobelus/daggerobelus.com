A Salve for sundry sores called

XXVII

Take one pt of oyle oliue of the best & of [w...te?]

Spanish sope incorporate all those well together in

a small fyre of coales contineweinge the said fyre an
houres & a halfe still stirring it w*i*th a sticke of [boxe?]

what [bigger?] till the reddnes be turned into a gray
colour but y*ou* must not leaue stirringe till the

this plaister laied to the stomacke [provokes?] appetite and
takes away the paines of the stomack laied to the
