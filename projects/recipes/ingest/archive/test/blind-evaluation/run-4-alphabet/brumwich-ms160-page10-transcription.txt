6

A water of Excelent vertue to helpe y*e* weake
or dimme sight of y*e* eyes when they
are decayed by age sicknes
or other way

Take 3 drames of tuetah in puder very smalle
& as much of aloes epaticum in puder 5 drames of
fine sugar 6 ounces of red rose water 6 ounces of
good white wine mixe all thes together & put it into
a cleane glasse being well closed & stoped & set
it in the sune a mounth together mixsing
& stiring together all the said things at least
ons a day to y*e* intent ya incorporate this
being done take of the same water & put one drope
in each eye morning & euening continue so & a
[Caron?] [scale?] it will cause the sight to come againe
& be as pure as it was before.
     + A water to Stringthen the sight
Take of wood bettony of 3 leaued grasse with the
white grasse prickley [Cristy?] yarow sinckfoild sallendine
red fenill of each a good handfull of eye bright 2 great
handfulls a handfull of white roses on the water of
them a quarter of a pinte shread all thes together &
lay them in steepe in halfe a pinte of white wine
& 2 or 3 spoonfulls of honey in an earthen pott
close stoped one whole night in y*e* morning still
[...] Distill it [halfe?] a pinte of
it in a common still adding to it [halfe?] [...] &
y*e* urine of a man child when you put it into
y*e* stille drinck a little of this in the morning
fasting 2 howers after it & with a litch rage
wash ye eyes
     + A powder for y*e* eyes
Take of y*e* seedes of Coriander prepared & sweet
fenill of each a drame of mace 2 scruples of eye
bright one ounce of fine sugar beaten & sifted
3 ounces make a powder of them take halfe a
spoonfull of it after diner & after supper

     + To Cuer a blind horse or [mistase?]
Take of the best & ~~[....]~~ wheat & lay it
Corne by Corne one a Clean [Reaser?] then take a
red hott fyre shouill & lay upon it & soe let
a man stand hand upon it & y*e* other will come
forth an oyle then take a feather quikley & [poke?]
up the oyle with it & soe draw the feather through
the eye. + A water for sore eyes whose lides are all [Swelled?]
Take a pritty quantity of red rosewater & as much Alowes
beaten to fine powder as will turne it & soe drope a
drope into y*e* eyes morning & euening & wash y*e* eye lides
with a feather

7

     + A Medicine for sore eyes

Take three Cloutes beat them [fine?] and [course?] them three Spoonsfulls
of fennill water 3 Spoonsfulls of [Mamsey?] 3 pennyworth of
[...] graces and [Balsame?] [...]  mixe all these in a little glasse
and [dresse?] y*e* eye with a feather once a day [...]  as y*e* [shall?]
see [occasion?]
     + for a pinne or pin & web in y*e* eye
the white of hens doung, and grated ginger, more of y*e* white y*e* of y*e* grager put some
into y*e* eye.
          A water for sore eyes comended by S*i*r James Langham

Take of water of frogs Spawn perdelicum half a pinte infuse into
it a dram of volus [metelorum?] after wait decant it of through a pece
of lawne paper after put into it of Salprunela & sugar Candy finely
powdred of each half an ounce after it has stood so 24 houres strein
it through a thick peice of brown paper & keep it for your use it is for
cooling y*e* eyes & preseruing & strengthing them & to take of specks
in y*e* eyes put in more sugar & Salprunela put some of this water
into a peice wide mouth [vioyell?] so open as to receive y*e* eye open
into it & so let y*e* water lye upon y*e* eye being opened some while this
[...] in a morning
          A very good receipt for sore eyes comended by M*r*s Worsley
Take a very good handfull of scabious half a pound of raisins of y*e*
sun stoned half a pound of blue corans bruised put thes into a galon of
mulling bear & drinke no other drinke & this has helpt many they
must at y*e* same tyme lay blistering plaisters behind y*e* ears
