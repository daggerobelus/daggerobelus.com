20

A Salve for sundry sores called
the Leaden plaister
     XXVII

Take one pt of oyle olive of the best & of red
lead very well seirced with dust 12 ounces of
Spanish sope incorporate all these well together in
an earthen pot well glased & when the ointm*en*t is
poured that the softe comes appermost & put it upon
a small face of coales continuowinge the said fire an
houres & a halfe still stirring it on a bolt of [boxe?]
fastned to a stick of wood, y*ou* make y*ou*r fire some
what bigger, till the reddish be turned into a grey
colour but y*ou* must not leave stirringe till the
matter be turned into the coulour of oyles somewhat
darker, then drop it on a wooden trencher & if it
nether cleave to y*ou*r fingers nor the trencher it is sufficient
enough, then dipps [plasters?] in that, the characters of
thereon dipped will last 20 yeares, pt of oyle & [halfe?]
this plaister laid to the Stomach [provokes?] appetite and
takes away the paines of the Stomack laid to the
side, tis a remedy for the [Collicke?] laid to the
raines of the back it stops the bloudy fluxe

openinge of the raines that of the Kidney or [worke?]s
of the back, it heals all swellings, Aches & [bruises?], it
[breaks?] [Fistoes?], Impostumes, [pushes?] & [byles?] them in
[springs?] applied to the [fundament?] it heales all diseases
thereon laid to the head, it is good for the [menigies?]

A Plod Cordiall for M*ist*r*ess* [Nowell?] in Childbed
     XXVIII

Take a quarter of a pint of Comfry water & [as?] m*u*ch of
Syrup of [Clovegillyflowers?] & a pennyworth of Cinnamon
water & a Spoonefull of [Gascon wine?] & M*ist*r*ess* [Darnells?]
Dram mingle all these together and it is a soveran
good cordiall for any especially for one that lyes
in Childbed.

     Pills for the Gall
     XXIX

     Two exceedinge good medecines and deuises
          after A Tetter

Take one Handfull of Sallanders & as much bay
salt as can take up betweene y*ou*r finger & Thumbe &
the iuice of halfe a Lemon stamp y*ou*r together in a
Mortar & straines the iuice out & [wash?] the place w*i*th
it put some of the heards upon it, use this 3 or 4
[dayes?] together dressing it 3 or 4 times in a Day
togeather &c.
