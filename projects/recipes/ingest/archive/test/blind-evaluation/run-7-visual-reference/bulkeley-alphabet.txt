Hand-Specific Alphabet: Bulkeley MS169, Page 17
================================================

This page is written in a mixed italic-secretary hand. The script is relatively
small but mostly legible, with a flowing quality. The text discusses lavender
and its medicinal properties. Page numbered 7 in upper right.

MINUSCULES (lowercase)
----------------------
a - Open form, sometimes angular; seen in "Lavander", "and", "that"
b - Ascender with bowl; seen in "be", "but", "blood"
c - Round, sometimes angular; seen in "Composition", "cold"
d - Secretary d with left-looping ascender; seen in "and", "distilled"
e - Mix of italic e and secretary e; seen in "the", "hele", "water"
f - Standard with crossbar; seen in "for", "flowers", "first"
g - Open descending tail; seen in "good", "great"
h - Sometimes loops below line; seen in "the", "that", "hote"
i - Simple minim; seen in "in", "it", "is"
k - Seen in "kindes"
l - Simple ascender; seen in "Lavander", "all"
m - Three minims; seen in "much", "many", "migram"
n - Two minims; seen in "not", "and", "none"
o - Round; seen in "of", "or", "one"
p - Standard; seen in "put", "parts"
q - With descender; seen in "qualitie"
r - Multiple forms; seen in "or", "for", "water"
s - Long-s medially, round s finally; seen in "smelt", "is", "roses"
t - Short vertical crossbar; seen in "the", "that", "to"
u/v - v initially, u medially; seen in "vnto", "vpon", "use"
w - Double-v; seen in "water", "was", "with"
x - Not prominently visible
y - With descender; seen in "yet", "your"
z - Not clearly visible

MAJUSCULES (uppercase)
----------------------
C - Curved; seen in "Composition"
L - Seen in "Lavander"
T - Seen in "The"
B - Seen in possible "But"

ABBREVIATIONS AND SPECIAL MARKS
---------------------------------
- Tilde (~) over vowels to indicate missing m/n: visible in some words
- Superscript letters for common abbreviations
- Long-s used consistently in medial position
- "&" (ampersand) used frequently
- The text uses "ye" for "the" (thorn)

CONFUSABLE LETTERS IN THIS HAND
---------------------------------
- e / o: In this hand, e tends to be more angular than o
- c / r: Can be similar
- long-s / f: Must check crossbar carefully
- m / n / u: Minim confusion possible throughout
- The mixed italic-secretary forms mean some letters have variant shapes
