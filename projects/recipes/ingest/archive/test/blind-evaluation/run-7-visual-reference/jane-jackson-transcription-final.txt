Note on overall quality:
Like the Brumwich manuscript, the Jane Jackson page remains extremely
challenging. The very high density of uncertain readings reflects genuine
difficulty with the small hand and (on the left page) severe water damage.
The vocab verification was able to confirm several readings and improve
confidence in some terms, but many passages remain uncertain or illegible.
The right page is somewhat clearer than the left but still contains many
difficult passages.
