Overall Assessment
------------------
The guided transcription is essentially a perfect match to the human
reference transcription from FromThePage. At 99.9% raw accuracy (100%
when confidence markers are stripped), the paleography guide enabled
Claude to produce a transcription that is word-for-word identical to
the human-produced reference.

Key observations:

- ZERO misreadings: Not a single word was read incorrectly. The only
  "difference" is an added confidence flag on a correctly-read word.

- All editorial conventions match: Damaged/obscured text markers,
  catchword notation, and special characters are all identical.

- Appropriate confidence flagging: The system flagged things that
  genuinely could benefit from human review without introducing any
  false readings.

- Challenging features handled correctly: Apothecary symbols, Roman
  numerals, early modern spellings, and abbreviations were all
  transcribed accurately.

This result validates the paleography guide approach for clearly-written
secretary hand manuscripts. The next step would be to test on more
difficult pages -- those with heavier damage, more compact hands, or
more extensive abbreviation -- to find the limits of this approach.
