# Funding Guide for the Recipe Books Project

A comprehensive guide to funding opportunities relevant to this project, organized by whether they provide personal income or computing resources/supplies.

---

## Fellowships and Grants That Provide Income

### GC Digital Fellows Program
- **Amount**: ~$30,179/year (GAB appointment + Provost's Award)
- **Deadline**: June 5 (check GCDI website for current cycle)
- **What it is**: A paid fellowship at the GC Digital Scholarship Lab. Fellows lead workshops, build digital projects, and conduct consultations. 450 non-teaching hours/year (~15 hrs/week).
- **Fit**: Excellent — directly aligned with DH work.
- **Important note on concurrent employment**: The stipend is split between a GAB payroll appointment (~$15K) and a Provost's Award through financial aid (~$15K). If you're on a GCF and currently teaching, this would likely REPLACE your teaching assignment for the year, not add to it. The 450 non-teaching hours max out the PSC-CUNY combined workload cap, leaving limited room for concurrent adjunct teaching. Some past fellows have held concurrent positions, but the specifics depend on your funding situation. Contact GCDI, the Office of Fellowships and Financial Aid, and your program's Executive Officer to clarify before applying.
- **Links**: https://gcdi.commons.gc.cuny.edu/ , https://digitalfellows.commons.gc.cuny.edu/about/fellowship/

### Mellon/ACLS Dissertation Innovation Fellowship
- **Amount**: $42,000 base stipend + up to $8,000 research/training/travel + $2,000 mentorship = up to $52,000
- **Duration**: One year (9-12 months)
- **Deadline**: ~October 29 (check ACLS website for current cycle)
- **Eligibility**: PhD students in humanities at U.S. institutions. Must have completed at least two years and all required coursework. Must NOT have advanced to ABD status prior to eligibility cutoff. English PhDs qualify. Digital humanities explicitly encouraged.
- **Restriction**: Cannot hold teaching or assistantship duties during the fellowship year.
- **Link**: https://www.acls.org/competitions/mellon-acls-dissertation-innovation-fellowship/

### GC Dissertation Fellowship
- **Amount**: Up to $25,000
- **Deadline**: ~January
- **What it is**: For students completing their dissertations.
- **Link**: https://www.gc.cuny.edu/fellowships-and-financial-aid/doctoral-student-funding/current-doctoral-student-funding

### Early Research Initiative (ERI) Fellowship
- **Amount**: $25,000 + in-state tuition
- **Eligibility**: Level II/III students
- **Link**: https://www.gc.cuny.edu/fellowships-and-financial-aid/doctoral-student-funding/early-research-initiative

### Provost's Pre-Dissertation Research Fellowship
- **Amount**: $4,000
- **Deadline**: ~February 3
- **What it is**: For humanities doctoral students developing dissertation proposals.
- **Link**: https://www.gc.cuny.edu/fellowships-and-financial-aid/doctoral-student-funding/early-research-initiative/internal-funding-sources

### Summer Public Research Fellowships (ERI + Center for the Humanities)
- **Amount**: $4,000
- **Deadline**: ~February
- **What it is**: Summer research on public-interest questions. Open to PhD students from any program.
- **Link**: https://www.gc.cuny.edu/fellowships-and-financial-aid/doctoral-student-funding/early-research-initiative/internal-funding-sources

### ACLS Digital Justice Grants
- **Amount**: Seed grants $10K-$25K; Development grants $50K-$100K
- **Deadline**: ~November (check ACLS website for current cycle)
- **Note**: These are project grants, not individual fellowships. Budgets CAN include salary if structured that way. Advanced doctoral students can serve as PIs.
- **Link**: https://www.acls.org/programs/acls-digital-justice-grants/

### NVIDIA Graduate Fellowship (Stretch Goal)
- **Amount**: Up to $60,000 (stipend + tuition + fees)
- **Deadline**: ~September 15
- **Catch**: Requires a mandatory in-person internship at an NVIDIA research office. Oriented toward CS/engineering. Worth knowing about but may not be practical.
- **Link**: https://research.nvidia.com/graduate-fellowships

## Grants for Computing Resources / Supplies (Not Income)

### Provost's Digital Innovation Grants (PDIGs)
- **Amount**: Up to $2,000 (equipment/services) + $300 (travel)
- **Deadline**: Watch GCDI website
- **What it can fund**: Cloud GPU time, equipment, digital services, technical support. NOT personal income.
- **Favors**: Open-source tools and publicly accessible work — strong fit for this project.
- **Link**: https://gcdi.commons.gc.cuny.edu/category/provosts-digital-innovation-grants/

### Doctoral Student Research Grant (DSRG)
- **Amount**: Up to $1,500
- **Eligibility**: Students in years 2-6
- **What it can fund**: Research expenses (could include cloud computing costs, Folger travel).
- **Link**: https://www.gc.cuny.edu/fellowships-and-financial-aid/doctoral-student-funding/early-research-initiative/internal-funding-sources

### Free GPU Access (No Application Needed)
- **Google Colab**: Free T4 GPU (16 GB VRAM). Students can get free Colab Pro: https://colab.research.google.com/signup
- **Kaggle Notebooks**: 30 free GPU hours/week (P100, 16 GB VRAM): https://www.kaggle.com

### CUNY High Performance Computing Center (HPCC)
- **Cost**: Free for CUNY grad students
- **Apply**: https://hpcreg1.csi.cuny.edu/forms/application.php
- **Contact**: hpchelp@csi.cuny.edu (ask about current GPU inventory)

### Cloud Credits (Application Required)
- **Google Cloud Research Credits**: $1,000/year. Rolling applications. https://edu.google.com/intl/ALL_us/programs/credits/research/
- **AWS Cloud Credit for Research**: Up to $5,000. Needs research proposal. https://aws.amazon.com/government-education/research-and-technical-computing/cloud-credit-for-research/
- **Microsoft Azure for Students**: $100 credits. https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/free/students

### NSF ACCESS (Explore Tier)
- **What**: Free national supercomputing (A100s, H100s). Computing time only, no cash.
- **Eligibility**: Grad students can apply as PI with advisor letter.
- **Link**: https://access-ci.org/

### NVIDIA Academic Grants
- **What**: Up to 30,000 H100 GPU hours. Hardware/credits only.
- **Deadline**: June 30
- **Note**: Applicants must be full-time faculty. Would need a faculty collaborator.
- **Link**: https://academicgrants.nvidia.com/

### Lambda Labs Research Grant
- **What**: Up to $5,000 in cloud credits + mentoring. Credits only.
- **Link**: https://lambdalabs.com/research

## Larger External Grants (Would Need Faculty PI or Post-PhD)

### NEH Digital Humanities Advancement Grants
- **Amount**: Up to $350,000 (Level I: $75K, Level II: $150K, Level III: $350K)
- **Note**: Institutional application — requires a faculty member as PI. Budget CAN include grad student salary. Has previously funded OCR/HTR projects.
- **Link**: https://www.neh.gov/grants/odh/digital-humanities-advancement-grants

### NEH Individual Fellowships
- **Amount**: Up to $60,000 ($5K/month)
- **Restriction**: NOT available to students currently enrolled in degree programs. Post-PhD only.
- **Link**: https://www.neh.gov/grants/research/fellowships
