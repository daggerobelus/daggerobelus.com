  To make a Thick Cream Cheese

take e new milk of 9 or 10 cows and 2 meals of Cream wch is about a pint of cream to a  Gallon of new milk & put hot water enough to it when you set it together to make it e warmth of milk when it comes from e cow & put 4 or 5 spoonfulls of quick Runet to it, when it is come, put about a quart of Boyling water upon it & let it stand to have e cream set in it about a quarter of an Hour, than take it up with e fleeting Dish break e curd as little as you can & lay it into e cheese & let it stand to whey, shake e cheese cloath about but dont press it much when it is wheyd gather e cheese cloath up & make it pretty Dry n have a clean cheese cloath in a mote with a Deep Hoose, & take it up with e fleeting Dish & lay it into e mote, when it is half in put a handfull of salt in e middle, when it is all in tuck e cloaths tite round & lay a cheese mote set about 14to pd weight upon it & when it has stood 2 Hours turn it upside down into e same mote again and let it stand with e weight upon it  till ? than turn it & rub some salt upon it & put it into a clean cloath & into e mote again n put it into e cheese press & pin a cloath e Debth of e cheese tite Round it & so keep shifting the cloath till e cheese is pretty Dry this cheese is best at a year old.
