## Source Type Explanation

- **FTP** (FromThePage): Crowd-sourced transcriptions of manuscript recipe books via the FromThePage platform. Good coverage but variable quality.
- **EMROC** (Early Modern Recipes Online Collective): Triple-keyed transcriptions where three people independently transcribe each page and discrepancies are resolved. Highest quality.
- **HERBAL** (Printed Herbals): OCR text from printed books (Gerard 1597, Culpeper 1652). These provide authoritative botanical and medical vocabulary. Note: OCR quality varies.

Words attested across multiple source types are more reliable.
